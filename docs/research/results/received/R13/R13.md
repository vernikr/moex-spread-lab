## 2. Executive Synthesis, Core Decision and Three-Layer Reframing

### 2.1 Bottom-Line Decision

**Default Recommendation — Conditional Deferral of Predictive Regime Layers:**  
As of 23 October 2026, incremental net value from regime conditioning on MOEX futures spreads is **unmeasured** in this project and **unsupported as a high-prior assumption** in the public empirical literature. No published academic study demonstrates out-of-sample, net-of-cost, integer-lot RUB excess over a money-market benchmark for regime-conditioned MOEX futures spreads. In adjacent international equities, FX, and commodities literature, in-sample regime dependence is pervasive, yet real-time out-of-sample conditioning frequently underperforms fixed-parameter unmanaged baselines due to state misclassification at transition boundaries, parameter instability, high turnover drag, and specification-search bias.

Accordingly:
1. **Do not adopt hidden-state (HMM/HSMM), change-point (BOCPD/CUSUM/PELT), or macro-forecasting layers now**, and introduce **no mandatory regime subsystem** into the screener or alert architecture.
2. **Enforce mandatory deterministic feasibility and policy masks (`R3`)** identically across both baseline and challenger runs; market closures, contract expiries, margin caps, and untradeable illiquid pairs are operational eligibility constraints, not evidence of predictive regime skill.
3. **Permit at most one bounded, preregistered Stage-2 falsification experiment** testing **simple, decision-time-observable risk/microstructure gates (`R2`)**—specifically trailing volatility, observable pair liquidity, and economically distinct curve-slope extremes—acting on **one isolated decision lever at a time** (entry permission first), **only after** the historical data-provenance gate and a frozen no-regime baseline (`Family C` and/or `Family M`) are established.
4. **Gate all latent/change-point challengers (`R1`) strictly behind `R2` survival:** only if a simple observable gate clears predeclared net-RUB, cost/delay stress, episode-diversity, and search-adjusted statistical hurdles—and leaves a concrete dynamic failure mode—may a single parsimonious causal filter (2-state forward-filtered HMM or online change-point detector) be tested against **both** the baseline and the best frozen simple gate. If simple gates fail, close R13 as *"no incremental evidence found; regime conditioning deferred."*

**Calibrated Confidence Assessment:** **High confidence** on deferring `R1`, on pilot sample-power arithmetic (§3.3), and on the policy-break nature of the MOEX sample (§5.2); **Moderate confidence** on testing extreme-only `R2` risk/feasibility gates (supported in international equities/futures, unproven on MOEX spreads); **Low / Unresolved** on all broker-specific margin (`нетто` vs. `полунетто`), commission, KPUR, and collateral facts (§14).

### 3.2 Matched LQDT Counterfactual Accounting & Primary Estimand

The benchmark instrument **LQDT** (*БПИФ РФИ «Ликвидность»*, managed by VIM Investitsii / VIYAM, ISIN `RU000A1014L8`, MOEX `ISSUEDATE` `2020-01-20`, Listing Level 1) is a RUB money-market exchange-traded fund investing in short-term CCP-cleared repo and cash instruments tracking the **RUSFAR** secured overnight rate.

For any strategy variant $v$ over horizon $t \in [0, T]$, let $A_{v,t}$ denote the marked-to-market value of the actual account path and $C_t$ denote the **matched LQDT counterfactual** path subjected to **identical true external cash contributions and withdrawals at identical timestamps**:

$$X_{v,t} = (A_{v,t} - C_t) - (A_{v,0} - C_0)$$

Under this formulation, the untouched portion of the > RUB 40M core LQDT position cancels identically between $A_{v,t}$ and $C_t$, while all pilot-attributable cash movements are captured cleanly:

$$X_{v,t} = \underbrace{\text{Realized PnL}_{v,t} + \text{Unrealized VM}_{v,t}}_{\text{Gross Futures Result}} - \underbrace{\left(\text{Exchange/Clearing Fees}_{v,t} + \text{Broker Commissions}_{v,t} + \text{Spread/Slippage/Legging}_{v,t}\right)}_{\text{Total Execution Friction}} + \underbrace{\left(R_{v,t}^{\text{actual cash/collateral}} - R_{t}^{\text{diverted LQDT counterfactual}}\right)}_{\text{Net Opportunity Cost / Collateral Carry}} - \Delta\text{Tax}_{v,t}^{\text{attrib}}$$

**Mandatory Accounting Rules:**
- **Counterfactual Retention:** Any core LQDT shares sold or cash diverted to fund the RUB 30,000 pilot (or approved emergency de-risking) remain continuously invested in LQDT inside $C_t$, compounding at LQDT's actual historical share/NAV return (not a synthetic CBR key-rate or raw RUSFAR/RUONIA proxy).
- **No Double-Counting of Retained Collateral:** If VTB/BCS permits core LQDT shares to serve as initial-margin collateral *without* selling them, those retained shares continue earning LQDT's NAV return inside $A_{v,t}$, canceling $C_t$'s return on those shares; only actual broker collateral haircuts, margin-lending fees, or cash diverted for variation margin incur net opportunity drag.
- **Tax Boundary:** Per project specification, exclude **only** any loss of the Russian long-term holding tax deduction (*ЛДВ*) on LQDT sales. Because investor tax residence and account tax regime are unknown, report **paired pre-tax net RUB excess** alongside explicit sensitivity notes for unresolved tax wedges rather than assuming all other taxes are zero.
- **Paired Incremental Estimand:** Each regime challenger $v$ is evaluated strictly against **its own frozen baseline family** $B \in \{\text{B-C}, \text{B-M}\}$:

$$\Delta X_{v,T} = X_{v,T} - X_{B,T}, \qquad d_{v,t} = \Delta X_{v,t} - \Delta X_{B,t}$$

Both the incremental difference $\Delta X_{v,T}$ and the absolute excess paths $(X_{v,T}, X_{B,T})$ must be reported: beating a negative-expectancy baseline while $X_{v,T} \le 0$ still fails versus holding LQDT.

#### B. Required Trade Count for Statistical Power
For a two-sided test at $\alpha = 0.05$ and $80\%$ power, the required number of **independent** round-trip trades to detect a per-trade incremental net improvement $\Delta$ given per-trade net excess standard deviation $\sigma$ is $N \approx 7.85 \cdot (\sigma / \Delta)^2$:

| Per-Trade Net Excess SD ($\sigma$, RUB) | $\Delta = \text{RUB } 100$ | $\Delta = \text{RUB } 150$ | $\Delta = \text{RUB } 200$ | $\Delta = \text{RUB } 400$ |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **$\sigma = \text{RUB } 200$** | 31 | 14 | 8 | 2 |
| **$\sigma = \text{RUB } 400$** | 126 | **56** | 31 | 8 |
| **$\sigma = \text{RUB } 800$** | 502 | 223 | 126 | 31 |

At realistic spread dispersion ($\sigma \approx \text{RUB } 400\text{--}600$, $\Delta \approx \text{RUB } 150$), roughly **60–125 independent round trips** (~90 completed round trips at $\sigma \approx 400, \Delta \approx 150$ under conservative tails) are required—equating to **12–24 months** at 4–8 trades/month *before* applying a cluster design-effect multiplier of **$2\times\text{ to }4\times$** for overlapping holdings and shared macro shocks across correlated contracts.

| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |

| **$N = 20$ configurations** | 24.0 years | **6.0 years** | 2.7 years |

Searching 20 regime configurations on ~4.5 years of post-February 2022 MOEX data places an apparent Sharpe 1.0 winner **inside the multiple-testing noise floor**. Therefore, the total R13 candidate configuration budget must be hard-capped ($\le 20$ configurations total, with $\le 8\text{--}12$ preregistered challenger cards), and every single failed parameter/threshold attempt must remain in the trial ledger.

## 4. Critical Synthesis of the Empirical and Econometric Evidence Base

English and Russian literature searches confirm **zero published peer-reviewed studies** testing regime-switching or change-point models specifically on MOEX futures calendar or cross-index spreads. Portable evidence from international equities, FX, energy/commodity futures, and econometric theory establishes four decisive priors:

### 4.1 Volatility Scaling, Latent State Filtering & Real-Time Failure Modes
- **In-Sample Optimism vs. Out-of-Sample Collapse:** Moreira & Muir (2017, *Journal of Finance* 72(4):1611–1644) reported substantial spanning-regression alphas and Sharpe gains from scaling equity-factor and currency-carry portfolios inversely to lagged realized variance. However, **Cederburg, O'Doherty, Wang & Yan (2020, *Journal of Financial Economics* 138(1):95–117)** tested real-time implementable walk-forward versions across **103 strategies** and found that out-of-sample volatility-managed portfolios generally earn **lower** certainty-equivalent returns and Sharpe ratios than unmanaged baselines (e.g., market Sharpe 0.42 managed vs. 0.46 unmanaged) due to structural instability in optimal scaling weights.
- **Why Latent Filters Struggle in Real Time:** **Ang & Timmermann (2012, *Annual Review of Financial Economics* 4:313–337)** demonstrate two foundational limits: (1) financial regimes are overwhelmingly identified by **conditional variance** ($\sigma_0 \ne \sigma_1$) while equality of regime-dependent means ($\mu_0 = \mu_1$) typically cannot be rejected—meaning volatility regimes provide *variance/risk* forecasts rather than directional return alpha; and (2) causal real-time filtered probabilities frequently miss true shifts or emit false alarms. **Dacco & Satchell (1999, *Journal of Forecasting* 18(1):1–16)** prove analytically that even a small state-misclassification rate destroys the forecasting advantage of a correctly specified regime-switching model over a parsimonious single-regime benchmark.
- **Flexible Latent Architectures Decay Out of Sample:** Empirical comparisons of Hidden Semi-Markov Models (HSMM) against standard HMMs on realized volatility (Ding, SSRN) and Value-at-Risk forecasting practitioners (*RiskHub*; Welch & Goyal 2008, *RFS* 21(4):1455–1508) consistently show that extra latent states or duration distributions improve in-sample likelihood while degrading out-of-sample stability, especially when crisis states contain few episodes.
- **The Narrow Exception That Survives:** **Bongaerts, Kang & van Dijk (2020, *Financial Analysts Journal* 76(4))** show that continuous volatility targeting suffers >200% annual turnover and target overshoot, increasing tail drawdowns; adjusting exposure **only in extreme volatility tails** reduces drawdowns and turnover because volatility clustering and negative return-volatility correlations concentrate in high-volatility states—though unlevered Sharpe gains were statistically significant only in US and Hong Kong markets. Similarly, the ECB *Financial Stability Review* (May 2018) documents persistent low/medium/high equity volatility clusters, motivating tail-risk gating rather than continuous fine-tuning.

### 4.3 Econometric Lineage for Causal Inference & Snooping Control
- **Causal Filtering vs. Retrospective Smoothing:** **Hamilton (1989, *Econometrica* 57(2):357–384; 2005 *Palgrave* manuscript pp. 5–7)** distinguishes *filtered* probabilities $P(S_t = k \mid \mathcal{F}_t)$ using observations up to $t$ from *smoothed* probabilities $P(S_t = k \mid \mathcal{F}_T)$ using future data $T > t$ (and notes that forward-filtering with full-sample-estimated parameters *still* leaks future information). **Bai & Perron (2003, *JAE* 18(1):1–22)** global residual-minimizing multiple structural breaks are strictly retrospective; causal deployment requires online sequential methods such as **Adams & MacKay (2007, `arXiv:0710.3742`)** Bayesian Online Changepoint Detection or recursive CUSUM—while training-only segmentation via **Killick, Fearnhead & Eckley (2012, `arXiv:1101.1438`)** PELT may only partition *past* training blocks. Label-switching remedies follow **Frühwirth-Schnatter (2006)** and **Jasra, Holmes & Stephens (2005, *Statistical Science* 20(1):50–67)**.
- **Data-Snooping & Nested-Comparison Controls:** Because standard holdouts ignore trial selection history (**Bailey et al. 2015/2017, *JCF***, proposing Combinatorially Symmetric Cross-Validation **`CSCV`** for `PBO`; **Bailey & López de Prado 2014, *JPM***), rigorous incremental evaluation draws on **White (2000, *Econometrica* 68(5):1097–1126, online 10 Dec 2003)** Reality Check, **Sullivan, Timmermann & White (1999, *JF* 54(5):1647–1691)**, **Hansen (2005, *Econometrica*)** Superior Predictive Ability (SPA), **Romano & Wolf (2005, *Econometrica*)** / **Romano, Shaikh & Wolf (2005)** stepdown FWER control, **Hansen, Lunde & Nason (2011, *Econometrica* 79(2):453–497)** Model Confidence Set (MCS), **Clark & West (2007, *J. Econometrics* 138(1):291–311)** nested MSPE adjustment, **Giacomini & Rossi (2010, *JAE* 25(4):595–620)** fluctuation/reversal tests, **Politis & Romano (1994, *JASA* 89(428):1303–1313)** stationary block bootstrap, **Harvey, Liu & Zhu (2016, *RFS* 29(1):5–68)** ($t > 3.0$ hurdle), **Bajgrowicz & Scaillet (2012, *JFE* 106(3):473–491)** (FDR + costs, showing investors could not select ex ante the future best-performing rules), and **Novy-Marx & Velikov (2016, *RFS* 29(1):104–147)** turnover-cost taxonomy. *(Note: `Bai & Perron 2003` carries a `2002` DOI copyright record.)*

### 5.1 Separation Rule: Exogenous Policy/Contract Breaks vs. Predictive Regimes
MOEX derivatives history is punctuated by administrative closures, sanctions-driven currency settlement switches, tariff revisions, and major 2025–2026 trading-session overhauls. ### 5.3 The "What Is a Day?" Problem, Contract Lifecycles & Margin Mechanics

1. **Timestamp-Anchored Bars vs. Session-Label Drift:** Because the `23.03.2026` unified session shifted evening trades (`19:00–23:50` MSK) from trading day $T+1$ into trading day $T$ and abolished legacy `19:00–19:05` MSK intermediate clearing, daily features computed naively on exchange `TRADEDATE` close labels suffer a structural seam. **Protocol Requirement:** Define all daily state snapshots by **explicit wall-clock timestamps in MSK (`UTC+3`)** (e.g., last completed bar or published settlement price confirmed received prior to decision slot $t$), respecting the historical session calendar effective on date $t$.
2. **Deterministic Expiry & Roll Calendars:** Quarterly MOEX index/FX futures traditionally expire mid-month (around the 15th business day of March, June, September, December), single-stock futures expire on the **third Thursday** of the contract month (settling the next trading day), energy/commodity contracts (`BR`, `NG`) follow monthly schedules, and post-`23.03.2026` cash-settled expirations occur intraday at `14:00` / `19:00` MSK. Basis compression and roll-week volume migration near expiration are **deterministic calendar mechanics**, not learned volatility/carry regimes.
3. **Exchange Calendar Spreads, Fee Discounts & NCC Margin Rules:**
   - MOEX lists native **synthetic calendar-spread instruments** (`Календарные спреды`, e.g., `MXI-9.24-12.24`, `OGI-6.26-9.26`, `Si-12.26-3.27`) executing both maturities simultaneously without leg-by-leg execution gap risk—though whether VTB/BCS exposes native spread order books for every contract on the user's account is unverified and must be modeled alongside manual two-leg execution.
   - **Exchange Fee Formula:** Per MOEX's calendar-spread specification, exchange fee per spread leg follows $\text{Fee}_{\text{CS}} = T \cdot F \cdot (1 - K)$ with discount factor $K = 0.2$ during the 6-month marketing period for non-OFZ underlyings (and $K = 0$ thereafter). Official FORTS base tariff rates by asset class (new edition effective `23.03.2026`, `moex.com/a90`) are: **FX `0.00154%`**, **Index `0.00220%`**, **Commodity `0.00440%`**, **Interest-rate `0.00550%`**, and **Equity `0.00660%`** of notional (plus NCC clearing fees and broker commission).
   - **Critical Unverified Margin Switch (`Нетто` vs. `Полунетто`):** While native calendar-spread order submission reserves initial margin equal to two atomic orders, NCC margin methodology (*Принципы расчета ГО*) specifies that ongoing calendar-spread margining follows either **net (`нетто` — interest-rate/calendar risk only)** or **semi-net (`полунетто` — the larger of the two legs' initial margins)** as *elected by the clearing member/broker* (`полунетто` applies by default on accounting registers). On a RUB 30,000 budget, mini-contracts like **MXI** (MOEX Russia Index mini) or **OGI** (Oil & Gas Index) carry single-leg margins of a few thousand RUB, whereas larger contracts (`Si`, `BR`, `MIX`, `RTS`) may fit 1 spread under `нетто` or 0–1 spreads under `полунетто`.

## 6. Point-in-Time Decision Clock, Data Provenance Gate and Required Field Schema

### 6.1 Four-Timestamp Chronology & Decision-Time Admissibility Rule
To eliminate look-ahead bias, revised-calendar backfill, and bar-completion leakage, every market, contract, and macroeconomic record $x$ must carry four distinct timestamps stored in both **Moscow Time (`MSK`, `UTC+3` / `GMT+3`)** and **`UTC`**:

1. **Observation / Event Time ($o_x$):** The historical session, candle interval, or economic reference period that $x$ measures.
2. **Issuer Publication / First-Seen Time ($p_x$):** The earliest verifiable instant a market participant could observe the value (e.g., Bank of Russia key-rate announcements are scheduled for **13:30 MSK** [`13.30` in schedule notation] on Board fixing days per the official *Publication Schedule*, though calendars carry a subject-to-change notice; MOEX evening settlement prices are published *after* determination during the evening session with a **15-minute free delay** per notice `n98831`, prohibiting use of day-$T$ settlement prices earlier on day $T$).
3. **Effective Time ($e_x$):** When a policy rate, exchange tariff edition (`moex.com/a90`), trading rule (`documents/301`), or contract specification legally governs trading (e.g., CBR key-rate tables mark rates as applying on every calendar day, yet a historical row dated `28.02.2022` does **not** establish that `20%` was known at `07:00 MSK` that morning).
4. **Research Retrieval Time & Snapshot Hash:** When the research pipeline fetched the file/URL, preserving the exact vintage text. Later-edited living pages (such as MOEX's `28.02.2022 13:12` updated notice or the `a8141` perpetuals product page) never prove earlier historical knowledge.

**Binding Admissibility Inequality:** At candidate decision timestamp $t$ (with latest confirmed data receipt $t^-$) followed by human/system execution latency $\Delta t_{\text{action}} \in [3, 90]$ minutes:

$$a_x \ge \text{Measurement Completion}(o_x) + \text{Documented Feed/Publication Delay} + \text{Acquisition Lag}, \qquad a_x \le t^- < t + \Delta t_{\text{action}}$$

Data feed latency and human reaction latency are **additive**: a 15-minute delayed ISS feed followed by a 15-minute manual reaction equals **30 minutes** of total price staleness, never 15 minutes. **Conservative Fallback Rule:** Wherever exact intraday publication/receipt timestamps cannot be reconstructed from dated archives, lag completed daily observations strictly to the **next trading session** or flag the state `unknown` (defaulting to no regime override).

## 7. Deterministic No-Regime Reference Baselines (Family C and Family M)

### 7.1 Universe Screening, Exclusions & Paired Comparison Rule
Regime conditioning must prove incremental value against a **nontrivial, deterministic, fixed-parameter spread baseline** trained under the exact same walk-forward splits—never against random trading or an ex-post oracle.
- **Strict Paired Comparison:** Every regime gate modifying `Family C` is compared solely against `Family C` (`B-C`), and every gate modifying `Family M` solely against `Family M` (`B-M`). `B-C` and `B-M` are evaluated as separate reference tracks; combining them requires a separate preregistered allocation rule counted against the trial budget.
- **Candidate Universe & Initial Exclusions:**
  - *Primary Same-Underlying Calendar-Spread Universe:* Dated MOEX FORTS futures families with at least two active expiries meeting trailing turnover floors and RUB 30,000 integer-lot margin feasibility—specifically mini/standard equity indices (`MXI`, `MIX`, `RTS`, `OGI`, `SPYF`), FX (`CNY`/`CNH`, and `Si` partitioned across the `13.06.2024` settlement break), commodities (`BR`/`BRM`, `GOLD`/`GD`, `NG`, `COCOA`), and liquid single-stock futures (`SBRF`, `GAZR`, `YDEX`) where margin permits. Mini-index contracts (`MXI`, `OGI`) are particularly suited to RUB 30,000 because single-contract GO sits in the low thousands of RUB (<50–60% of pilot capital), whereas position-concentration tiers on `MIX`/`RTS` are non-binding at 1–2 pilot lots.
  - *Optional Cross-Contract Mean-Reversion Universe:* Economically linked RUB-denominated index/sector pairs (e.g., front-month Oil & Gas `OGI` vs. MOEX Russia Index mini `MXI`)—tested separately from same-underlying calendars because cross-underlying spreads introduce OLS/Johansen hedge-ratio estimation variance and multiplier-mismatch risk.
  - *Mandatory Initial Exclusions:* **Perpetual futures** (`USDRUBF`, `EURRUBF`, `IMOEXF`) due to daily auto-prolongation and funding-rate mechanics; contracts with unresolved specification jumps; and any spread whose minimum integer lot plus stress variation-margin reserve exceeds **RUB 30,000 with zero emergency funding**.

### 7.2 Baseline Family 1 (`B-C`): Deterministic Fixed-Parameter Carry / Calendar Spread
Targets term-structure roll yield / mispricing across adjacent or next-quarter maturities $(T_1 < T_2)$ of the same underlying using strictly training-fitted static parameters:

1. **Primary Curve-Slope Signal (Model-Free Annualized Log Basis):** For strictly positive, unit-compatible futures prices observed synchronously at $t^-$:
   $$s_{p,t} = \frac{365}{T_2 - T_1} \ln\!\left(\frac{F_{p,t}^{(2)}}{F_{p,t}^{(1)}}\right) \quad \left(\text{equivalently normalized linear carry } b_{p,t} = \frac{F_{p,t}^{(2)} - F_{p,t}^{(1)}}{F_{p,t}^{(1)}} \cdot \frac{365}{T_2 - T_1}\right)$$
   *(Consistent with Chan's constant-roll calendar-spread model; where observable point-in-time funding $r_t$, storage $c_t$, and convenience yield $y_t$ exist, an optional cost-of-carry mispricing form $S_t^{\text{carry}} = F_{t,T_2} - F_{t,T_1}\exp[(r_t + c_t - y_t)(T_2 - T_1)]$ may be preselected instead, never both.)*
2. **Entry Rule:** Enter 1 integer spread unit (short the relatively expensive maturity: **Long Near $T_1$ / Short Far $T_2$** when $s_{p,t} \ge +k_{\text{entry}}^{\text{train}}$, and **Short Near $T_1$ / Long Far $T_2$** when $s_{p,t} \le -k_{\text{entry}}^{\text{train}}$, where $k_{\text{entry}}^{\text{train}}$ is fixed on training data—e.g., $Q_{0.75}(|s|)^{\text{train}}$ or $\mu_S \pm k_{\text{entry}}\sigma_S$).
3. **Exit Rule:** Close upon basis convergence ($|s_{p,t}| \le k_{\text{exit}}^{\text{train}}$), fixed holding horizon $H$ (e.g., $H \in \{5, 10\}$ or $10\text{--}20$ trading sessions selected in training), or mandatory pre-expiry cutoff (e.g., $\ge 5$ sessions prior to $T_1$ expiration), whichever comes first. **Total free parameters: 2–3 (frozen per fold).**

### 7.3 Baseline Family 2 (`B-M`): Deterministic Fixed-Parameter Mean Reversion
Locked prior to testing in one of two sub-forms depending on the chosen trade object:

- **Sub-Form `B-M1` (Same-Underlying Calendar-Basis Mean Reversion):** Standardize $s_{p,t}$ against its training/trailing rolling window $w$: $z_{p,t} = (s_{p,t} - \mu_{p,\text{train}}) / \sigma_{p,\text{train}}$. Enter 1 integer spread opposing the deviation when $|z_{p,t}| \ge z_{\text{in}}$ (e.g., $z_{\text{in}} \in \{1.5, 2.0\}$); exit when $|z_{p,t}| \le z_{\text{out}}$ (e.g., $0.5$), at fixed maximum holding $H \in \{5, 10\}$ sessions, or at pre-expiry roll cutoff. **(3–4 parameters.)**
- **Sub-Form `B-M2` (Cross-Contract Ornstein–Uhlenbeck Cointegrated Spread):** Model spread residual $S_t = P_{1,t} - \beta P_{2,t} - \alpha$ (with $\beta, \alpha$ estimated via OLS or Johansen cointegration on training windows only) as a continuous-time OU process:
  $$dS_t = \theta (\mu - S_t)\,dt + \sigma\,dW_t, \qquad t_{1/2} = \frac{\ln(2)}{\theta}, \qquad Z_t = \frac{S_t - \mu}{\sigma / \sqrt{2\theta}}$$
  Hold parameter vector $\boldsymbol{\psi} = \{\theta, \mu, \sigma, \beta\}$ strictly constant across each out-of-sample fold; enter at $|Z_t| \ge 2.0$, exit at $|Z_t| \le 0.5$ or deterministic time stop $t_{\text{max}} = 2 \cdot t_{1/2}$.

## 8. Taxonomy of Candidate Regime States and Point-in-Time Formulations

Candidate regime indicators follow a strict complexity hierarchy: **Simple Observable States (`R2`)** computed closed-form from trailing data up to $t^-$ are tested first; **Latent / Change-Point Models (`R1`)** are deferred strictly behind `R2` survival.

### 8.1 Simple Observable States: Volatility, Liquidity & Carry/Funding

#### A. Observable Volatility States (`V`)
1. **Spread-Level Trailing Realized Volatility (`V-Spread`):** Annualized standard deviation $\hat{\sigma}_{p,t}$ of completed settlement-to-settlement spread changes over trailing $n = 20$ sessions (or $30$ sessions). Partitioned strictly against the trailing training window (e.g., 200, 252, or 504 sessions) into either:
   - *Extreme-Only Binary Gate (`V-Ext` — highest literature prior per Bongaerts et al. 2020):* $\mathbb{I}\{\hat{\sigma}_{p,t} \ge Q_{0.90}^{\text{train}}(\hat{\sigma}_p)\}$ (or $Q_{0.75}^{\text{train}}$ / $Q_{0.80}^{\text{train}}$ / median $Q_{0.50}^{\text{train}}$, locked ex ante—never swept).
   - *Three-State Tercile / Quantile Split:* Low ($< Q_{0.40}$), Normal ($[Q_{0.40}, Q_{0.80}]$), High/Crisis ($> Q_{0.80}$).
2. **Splice-Free Instrument-Family Realized Volatility (`V-Family`):** For each underlying $j$ in family $f$, compute daily log returns $r_{j,t}$ **strictly within the same contract maturity** (never splicing prices across a contract roll jump):
   $$v_{j,t} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{20}\sum_{k=0}^{19} r_{j,t-k}^2}, \qquad V_{f,t} = \operatorname{median}_{j \in f}\!\left(\frac{v_{j,t}}{\sigma_j^{\text{train}}}\right)$$
   where $\sigma_j^{\text{train}}$ is frozen on the training fold. High family volatility triggers when $V_{f,t} > Q_{0.80}^{\text{train}}(V_f)$.
3. **Parameter-Free Short/Long Realized Volatility Ratio (`R_vol`):**
   $$R_{\text{vol},t} = \frac{\sigma_{\text{short},t}(N_s = 5)}{\sigma_{\text{long},t}(N_l = 60)} = \frac{\sqrt{\frac{252}{5}\sum_{i=0}^{4} r_{t-i}^2}}{\sqrt{\frac{252}{60}\sum_{j=0}^{59} r_{t-j}^2}}$$
   Partitioned deterministically into Compressed ($< 0.75$), Normal ($[0.75, 1.50]$), and High Volatility ($> 1.50$).
4. **Market-Wide Volatility (`V-Market-Broad` vs. `V-Market-RVI`):**
   - *Leave-Family-Out Broad Realized Volatility (`V-Broad`):* Median of standardized $V_{f',t}$ across all families $f' \ne f$, requiring $\ge 3$ valid external families (otherwise flagged `unavailable`), isolating cross-market information free of own-family contamination.
   - *MOEX Russian Volatility Index (`V-RVI`, analogous to `VIX`):* 30-day RTS-options implied volatility (published every 15s on MOEX and mirrored on public portals such as *Investing.com*—always source from official MOEX archives; use print $\le 18:45\text{ MSK}$ or prior session close vs. trailing 252-session $Q_{0.80}^{\text{train}}$; `IMOEX` index serves only for descriptive context). *Mandatory Caveat:* RVI reflects equity-index option skew (methodology updated `23.03.2026`) and may not transfer cleanly to commodity or FX spreads (Marshall et al. 2013).

#### B. Observable Liquidity & Market-Access States (`L`)
1. **Weaker-Leg RUB Turnover Ratio (`L-Turnover`):** Using completed-session RUB turnover $U_{i,t}$ on each leg $i \in \{1, 2\}$:
   $$L_{i,t} = \frac{\operatorname{median}(U_{i,t-4:t})}{\operatorname{median}(U_{i,t-59:t})}, \qquad L_{p,t} = \min(L_{1,t}, L_{2,t})$$
   Low-activity flag triggers when $L_{p,t} < Q_{0.20}^{\text{train}}(L_p)$ (or prior-session family turnover $< Q_{0.25}^{\text{train}}$). Zero denominators or missing contract history map to `unknown`, never an artificial extreme.
2. **Calendar-Spread Trade-Count, Volume & Quoted-Width Floor (`L-Pair`):** Trailing 20-day (or prior-day against 120–252d history) median spread trade-count floor (e.g., $\ge 10\text{–}30$ trades/session and volume $> Q_{0.20\text{–}0.30}^{\text{train}}$) combined—**only if archived contemporaneous quotes exist**—with quoted bid–ask width $< Q_{0.50\text{–}0.80}^{\text{train}}$.
3. **Order-Book Depth Composite (`L_depth`, Conditional on Type C / Licensed Quotes):**
   $$L_t^{\text{book}} = \frac{P_t^{\text{ask}} - P_t^{\text{bid}}}{P_t^{\text{mid}}} \times \frac{1}{\text{Depth}_t^{\text{top5}}}, \qquad \text{Impaired if } L_t^{\text{book}} > Q_{0.75}^{\text{train}}$$
   *Downgrade & Non-Double-Counting Rules:* If historical timestamped quotes are absent, downgrade strictly to `L-Turnover`/`L-Pair` trade counts (never invent zero spreads); do not erase baseline bid–ask slippage on remaining trades or double-count spread width as both cost savings and gross alpha.

#### C. Observable Carry, Term-Structure & Funding States (`C` / `M`)
1. **Curve-Slope / Contango–Backwardation Quantiles (`C-Curve`):** Classify $s_{p,t}$ (or $b_{p,t}$) against its trailing training distribution—e.g., extreme adverse tails outside central 80% ($< Q_{0.10}^{\text{train}}$ or $> Q_{0.90}^{\text{train}}$), terciles, strong backwardation ($< Q_{0.20}^{\text{train}}$) vs. strong contango ($> Q_{0.80}^{\text{train}}$), or high absolute carry ($|c_t| \ge Q_{0.67}^{\text{train}}$). **Strict Non-Redundancy Rule:** Because `B-C` already trades on $s_{p,t}$, any `C-Curve` gate applied to `B-C` must demonstrate value beyond a simple adjustment of `B-C`'s own entry threshold $k_{\text{entry}}$ (or be restricted to conditioning `B-M` or interacting with extreme volatility `C × V-Ext`).
2. **Monetary Policy & Money-Market Carry Proxies (`M-Rate`):** Point-in-time CBR key-rate stance (tightening / easing / stable based on released Board decisions, lagged to next session unless `13:30 MSK` timestamp is verified) or overnight money-market basis $M_t = \text{RUSFAR}_t - \text{CBR Key Rate}_t$ (or `RUONIA`; broader `OFZ` yield-curve and first-published CPI inflation regimes are restricted strictly to evaluation strata, never revised-data trading inputs). *Caveats:* `RUONIA` measures unsecured interbank RUB rather than retail margin financing or LQDT yield, and raw interest-rate levels largely rescale both futures basis and the LQDT counterfactual simultaneously—making `M-Rate` a secondary family-specific control rather than a primary alpha filter.

### 8.2 Deferred Stage-3 Latent-State & Change-Point Models (`R1`)

Tested **only if** at least one simple `R2` gate survives walk-forward validation and sample-adequacy checks:

1. **Two-State Causal Gaussian Hidden Markov Model (`HMM-2` / `SWARCH`):**
   - *Specification:* Univariate input (log family volatility $\ln V_{f,t}$, AR(1) spread changes, or spread return $r_{p,t}$; bivariate $(r_{p,t}, \hat{\sigma}_{p,t})$ or Switching ARCH `SWARCH` optional only if sample permits), $S_t \in \{1, 2\}$ with transition matrix $\mathbf{P} = (p_{ij})$ and Gaussian emissions $\mathcal{N}(\mu_k, \sigma_k^2)$ fitted via EM **strictly on each training fold** using a fixed small number of random initializations.
   - *Causal Forward Filtering Only:* Decisions at $t$ use **one-step forward-filtered probabilities** $\alpha_t(k) = P(S_t = k \mid \mathcal{F}_{t^-})$ exclusively (e.g., vetoing when $\alpha_t(\text{high-vol}) \ge 0.80$ or requiring favorable-state probability $P(S_t = 1 \mid \mathcal{F}_{t^-}) > 0.70$). Full-sample Baum–Welch smoothed probabilities $P(S_t = k \mid \mathcal{F}_T)$ and global Viterbi paths are **strictly prohibited** in live/out-of-sample simulation (used solely as an invalid look-ahead diagnostic).
   - *Label-Switching Anchor:* Anchor State 2 deterministically as the higher-volatility (or weaker mean-reversion) state via $\sigma_2^{\text{train}} > \sigma_1^{\text{train}}$ (or ordered emission mean) inside each training fold—never by ex-post trading profitability. If emission separation is indistinct or flips across restarts, abort fit and fall back to no override.
2. **Bayesian Online Changepoint Detection (`BOCPD` — Adams & MacKay 2007):**
   - *Recursion:* Tracks run length $r_t$ (bars elapsed since the last regime break) causally via:
     $$P(r_t = k \mid \boldsymbol{x}_{1:t}) \propto \sum_{r_{t-1}} P(r_t \mid r_{t-1})\, P\!\left(x_t \mid r_{t-1}, \boldsymbol{x}_t^{(r)}\right) P(r_{t-1} \mid \boldsymbol{x}_{1:t-1})$$
     using conjugate priors fitted on training data and constant hazard $H(k) = 1/\lambda$ (e.g., $\lambda = 60$ sessions).
   - *Critical Constant-Hazard Reconciliation:* Under exact constant hazard $H(k) = 1/\lambda$ prior to observing $x_{t+1}$, the posterior reset mass $P(r_t = 0 \mid \boldsymbol{x}_{1:t})$ reduces identically to $1/\lambda$! Therefore, rather than naively thresholding $P(r_t = 0) > 0.65$ (which is non-degenerate only under non-constant hazards or post-predictive conventions), define the **recent-break probability mass**:
     $$R_t = P(1 \le r_t \le 5 \mid \boldsymbol{x}_{1:t}) > Q_{0.90}^{\text{train}}(R)$$
     Audit sensitivity to serial persistence (since BOCPD assumes i.i.d. observations within segments) and document any low-mass run-length tail pruning (`Adams & MacKay 2007`, p. 4).
3. **Recursive CUSUM Forecast-Residual Filter (`CUSUM`):** Monitors 1-step-ahead baseline forecast residuals $e_i$ via $W_t = \frac{1}{\sigma_{\text{train}}\sqrt{T_{\text{win}}}} \sum_{i=1}^{t} (e_i - \bar{e}_{\text{train}})$, signaling instability when $|W_t| > c_{\alpha}$ (e.g., $\alpha = 0.05$).
4. **Training-Window PELT Segmentation (`PELT` — Killick et al. 2012):** Applies Pruned Exact Linear Time penalized segmentation **strictly inside each historical training window** to calibrate baseline parameters $(\mu, \sigma, \theta)$ on the most recent completed stationary epoch—never applying retrospective full-sample **Bai & Perron (2003)** breakpoints across test periods.

| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Observable (`R2`)** | Volatility Ratio (`R_vol`) | 0 (fixed cutoffs) | 5d / 60d | $< 1\text{ ms}$ | $< 1\text{ MB}$ | Very Low |
| **Observable (`R2`)** | Family / Spread Vol (`V`) or `RVI` | 1–3 (window, quantile) | 20d / 252–504d | $< 1\text{ ms}$ | $< 1\text{ MB}$ | Low |

## 9. Transmission Channels, Single-Lever Isolation and Master Hypothesis Registry

### 9.1 Architectural Isolation: Permitted vs. Prohibited Transmission Channels
Changing entry thresholds, position sizes, exit stops, holding durations, and pair universes simultaneously guarantees in-sample overfitting and makes attribution impossible. **Each primary hypothesis card may alter exactly ONE operational lever**, keeping all other baseline rules frozen:

| Transmission Lever | Permitted Predeclared Action | Prohibited Action | Priority Tier |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Pair / Universe Eligibility** | Suspend new entries in pairs failing trailing turnover/quote floors, RUB 30,000 GO feasibility, or scheduled CBR/holiday masks (`R3`/`L`). | Rotating ad hoc into unscreened asset classes or dropping pairs based on ex-post P&L. | **Tier 0 / Tier 1 (Primary)** |
| **2. Entry Permission / Threshold** | Binary entry suppression (veto fresh entries in adverse state while existing trades follow baseline exits) or fixed-step entry $z$-score widening (e.g., $+0.5\sigma$). | Retroactively skipping losing trades, continuous threshold curve-fitting, or mixing entry + exit changes in Stage 1. | **Tier 1 (Primary Isolation)** |
| **3. Position Sizing Cap** | Discrete step-down (e.g., $w_t \in \{1.0, 0.5, 0.0\}$ integer-rounded) in high-vol or mid-liquidity states *where baseline holds $\ge 2$ contracts*. | Continuous leverage scaling ($1/\hat{\sigma}_t^2$), exceeding RUB 30,000, or disguising an entry veto via single-lot rounding (see *Note* below). | **Tier 2 (Secondary Ablation)** |
| **4. Exit / Holding Duration** | Mandatory early market exit on online break alarm or shortened max hold during pre-announced policy transition weeks. | Shifting stop-losses ex post or extending losing holds in "favorable" states without turnover accounting. | **Tier 2 (Secondary Ablation)** |
| **5. Training Parameter Epoch** | Estimate baseline $(\mu, \sigma, \theta)$ on most recent training-only `PELT` block rather than full rolling window. | Full-sample breakpoint segmentation (`Bai–Perron`) or continuous test-window refitting. | **Tier 3 (Optional Phase 2)** |

> **Critical Integer-Lot Sizing Caveat at RUB 30,000:** Under integer contract constraints on a RUB 30,000 account, whenever the baseline holds **1 spread unit**, a sizing multiplier of $w_t = 0.5$ rounds down to **$0$ contracts**—making "50% position sizing" mathematically identical to a **binary entry veto** while risking high fixed-commission drag if multi-lot small contracts (`MXI`) are truncated mid-trade. Sizing cards (`S1`) are therefore valid as distinct hypotheses **only** on contracts where RUB 30,000 holds $\ge 2$ units!

| **`G7-Rate`** | **Tier 1/2:** Entry Only | Point-in-time CBR key-rate / carry proxy implies net spread edge after costs $\le \text{floor}$ (or tightening stance). | Suppress `B-C` entries when financing shifts erode net carry edge. | Captures overnight rate-regime shifts. *Falsified if* effect relies solely on Feb 2022 or adds nothing beyond `B-C` basis + LQDT benchmark scaling. |
| **`G8-Comb`** | **Tier 2:** Two Simple Gates | Predeclared pair of surviving Tier-1 gates (e.g., `Vol + Liq` or `Vol + Carry`). | Apply both component gates without re-tuning thresholds. | Tests complementarity of volatility and liquidity/carry filters. *Falsified if* fails to beat best single component gate after DSR/MCS penalty. |
| **`S1-Size`** | **Tier 2:** Size Only | `V-Spread` tercile or `L_t` `[P50, P75]` moderate impairment on multi-lot contracts (`MXI`/`OGI`). | Scale integer contracts $2 \to 1$ ($w_t = 0.5$); keep entry/exit fixed. | Stabilizes per-trade RUB variance without full entry blackout. *Falsified if* integer rounding truncates $>50\%$ of signals to 0 lots or fee drag rises $>25\%$ of gross. |
| **`S2-Exit`** | **Tier 2:** Exit Only | High-vol transition or online `CUSUM`/`BOCPD` break alarm while position is open. | Accelerate exit / shorten max holding stop; entry unchanged. | Cuts holding exposure through structural breaks. *Falsified if* false alarms cause whipsaw turnover losses exceeding avoided drawdowns. |
| **`L1-HMM`** | **Tier 3 (Deferred):** Entry Only | 2-state causal forward-filtered Gaussian HMM ($\alpha_t(\text{high-vol}) \ge 0.80$, emission-ordered on training). | Identical entry-only veto as best surviving `G1`/`G2` comparator. | Tests whether latent transition persistence improves upon raw rolling quantiles. *Falsified if* fails to beat **both** `H0` and best simple gate, or label-flip rate $>20\%$. |
| **`L2-CP`** | **Tier 3 (Deferred):** Entry Only | Online `BOCPD` recent-change mass $R_t = P(1 \le r_t \le 5 \mid \boldsymbol{x}_{1:t}) > Q_{0.90}^{\text{train}}$ *(using $\mid$ safe notation: `P(1 <= r_t <= 5 given x_1:t)`)* or recursive `CUSUM` $\lvert W_t \rvert > c_{0.05}$. | Entry-only veto during post-break transient window. | Detects abrupt distributional shifts faster than 20d rolling windows. *Falsified if* alarm lags 3–90 min execution reality, stays near-constant, or loses to `G1`/`G2`. |
| **`L3-PELT`** | **Tier 3 (Deferred):** Training Epoch | Training-only `PELT` segmentation (`Killick et al. 2012`) on spread/volatility history. | Estimate fixed fold parameters $(\mu, \sigma, \theta)$ on latest completed training segment only. | Avoids contaminating baseline mean/variance with stale pre-break training regimes. *Falsified if* sensitive to penalty $\beta_{\text{pen}}$ or no OOS gain vs. rolling window. |

*(Explicitly Logged as **Rejected-by-Default** prior to data contact to preserve audit completeness: multi-state ($\ge 3$) or multivariate macro HMMs/MS-GARCH/HSMMs; simultaneous multi-lever optimization; low-volatility holding-time extensions; and retrospective full-sample `Bai–Perron` break labeling.)*

## 10. Chronological Walk-Forward Skeleton and 15-Point Leakage Protocol

### 10.1 Walk-Forward Architecture, Overlap Purging, Roll Embargoes & Untouched Holdout

All baseline parameters, volatility/liquidity/carry quantiles, hedge ratios, and (if reached) latent transition matrices must be estimated **strictly inside historical training folds** using chronological walk-forward splits (no K-fold cross-validation, no random shuffling):

1. **Candidate Split Parameterizations (Lock One Based on Verified Sample Depth at Step 0):**
   - *Protocol Option A (Multi-Year Daily Panel Default):* **504 trading sessions** (~2 years) rolling training window $\to$ **interval-purged embargo** $\to$ **63 trading sessions** (~1 quarter) frozen out-of-sample test block $\to$ **252 trading sessions** (~12 contiguous months) untouched final holdout.
   - *Protocol Option B (Shorter Homogeneous History):* **250 trading sessions** (~1 year, expanding or rolling) training window $\to$ **purging + embargo** $\to$ **60 trading sessions** (~1 quarter) frozen test block $\to$ **6–12 contiguous months** untouched final holdout.
2. **Exact Event-Interval Purging & Roll-Aligned Embargoes:** Fixed arbitrary gaps (e.g., 5 days) fail if positions are held longer or roll across expiries. The train–test gap must remove every training decision whose **signal-to-actual-exit interval** (including holding horizon $H$ or $t_{\text{max}} = 2 t_{1/2}$, partial-fill de-risking, and 90-minute execution delay) overlaps the test period, **plus** a serial-correlation embargo (at least $\max(H + 1\text{ session}, 5\text{ sessions})$) aligned to **FORTS contract-roll boundaries** so a training fold never terminates inside a front contract's expiry week.
3. **Fold Warm-Up & Parameter Freeze:** Trailing features may initialize using past pre-test observations already available at $t^-$, or run the first $\max(\text{window}, \text{lookback})$ bars as baseline-only in both arms; however, **all fitted thresholds, scales, and state mappings remain strictly frozen throughout each test block** (recomputed only at scheduled fold boundaries).
4. **The Post-`14.09.2026` Clean-Holdout Constraint & One-Shot Rule:** Because MOEX redefined trading-day/clearing boundaries on `23.03.2026` and session hours on `14.07.2026` / `14.09.2026` (§5.2), any historical holdout spanning pre-September 2026 straddles structural session changes, whereas a strictly homogeneous modern holdout begins **`14.09.2026`** and must accumulate forward in calendar time. Regardless of start date, the **final holdout is cryptographically hashed, access-logged, and evaluated exactly ONCE** after freezing baseline, single nominated challenger, cost/delay rules, and hurdles. A failed holdout may **never** be recycled into a new training set.

### 10.2 Two-Leg Delayed Order-State Machine & Transition Accounting
Even for entry-only gates, state transitions are **not frictionless**:
- **Delayed Two-Leg Execution Machine:** Every eligible signal triggers an alert at $t$, advances by $\Delta t_{\text{action}} \in [3, 90]$ minutes, checks session status (halt, clearing, close, contract expiry) and quote freshness, and executes either on the native MOEX calendar-spread book (if verified accessible) or as two atomic leg orders—explicitly tracking **filled**, **partially filled (legged)**, **rejected**, **canceled**, and **stale** outcomes, along with emergency de-risking costs on unhedged partial legs and daily variation-margin calls.
- **State-Churn & Alert Accounting:** Log every state-permission flip, suppressed entry, subsequent capital reallocation effect on later signals, fold-boundary threshold jump, model fit failure, and human Telegram-alert intervention. Adding post-hoc hysteresis bands, cooldown timers, or state-smoothing rules after observing state whipsaw (>3 flips/day) counts as a **new trial configuration** against the 20-trial cap.

### 10.3 Mandatory 15-Point Leakage & Integrity Audit Suite

Every baseline and regime run must pass **all 15 verification checks** before out-of-sample statistics may be inspected (any material failure invalidates the run and triggers pipeline correction):

| # | Integrity / Leakage Test | Audit Procedure | Mandatory Pass Criterion |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **L1** | **Strict Timestamp Inequality** | Assert `published_at + feed_delay <= decision_time` and `outcome_start >= decision_time + action_delay` across 100% of rows. | **0 violations**; offending fields dropped or lagged to next session. |
| **L2** | **Prefix Invariance (Future-Blind Perturbation)** | Truncate dataset at $t$ (or overwrite all records $> t$ with random noise) and recompute state/signal at $t$. | Historical state labels and trade decisions at $\le t$ match **bit-for-bit (100%)**. |

| **L15** | **Manual Raw-File Trade Reconstruction** | Recompute a random sample of **200 simulated trades** (signals, delays, fees, VM, LQDT diff) by hand/independent script from raw archives. | **100% exact reconciliation**; halt and repair pipeline on any discrepancy. |

## 11. Sequential Ablation Ladder, Dependence-Aware Uncertainty and Multiple-Testing Controls

### 11.1 Ordered Ablation Ladder & Five-Part Portfolio-Path Decomposition

A common methodological error in published regime studies is comparing a conditioned strategy directly against an unscreened raw baseline, misattributing simple tradability/feasibility cleanup to "predictive regime alpha." **Every rung on the R13 ablation ladder must beat the immediately preceding surviving rung**, holding all other rules identical:

| Rung | Model Configuration | Comparator | Isolation Purpose & Decision Rule |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **`A0`** | Frozen No-Regime Baseline (`B-C` or `B-M`) + deterministic `R3` session/expiry/holiday masks (`F3-Cal`) | Matched `LQDT` ($C_t$) | Establishes unconditional reference path across the full $3 \text{ Cost} \times 5 \text{ Delay}$ grid. |
| **`A1`** | `A0` + Liquidity Pair-Eligibility Gate (`F1-Liq`) | `A0` | Isolates removal of untradeable (`27%` zero-trade) and ultra-wide-spread pairs. |
| **`A2`** | `A1` + Initial-Margin / RUB 30,000 Feasibility Gate (`F2-GO`) | `A1` | Defines the **true executable no-regime baseline (`A2`)** using 0 statistical parameters. |
| **`A3`** | `A2` + **One** Primary Observable Entry Gate (`G1-VolF`, `G2-VolExt`, `G4-Turn`, `G5-Carry`, or `G7-Rate` run separately) | **`A2`** | **First genuine regime test:** must improve paired net RUB excess over `A2`, not merely `A0`. |
| **`A4`** | Scope, Redundancy & Controlled Interaction Checks:<br>• `G3-VolM` (Leave-Family-Out Broad Vol / `RVI`) vs. `G1-VolF`<br>• `G5-Carry` vs. simple `B-C` entry-threshold shift<br>• `G6-CxV` or `G8-Comb` (1 predeclared pair) vs. best `A3`<br>• Secondary single-lever `S1-Size` or `S2-Exit` vs. `A3` | Best **`A3`** winner (or shifted `A2`) | Verifies whether market-wide data beats family data, whether carry state is merely baseline signal re-thresholding, and whether a second lever/state justifies extra degrees of freedom. |
| **`A5`** | *Conditional Stage 3 Only:* `A2` + Causal `L1-HMM`, `L2-CP`, or `L3-PELT` on the identical entry lever | **Both `A2` and best frozen `A3`/`A4`** | Latent complexity must outperform the best simple observable gate after search and turnover penalties; otherwise discard. |

#### Mandatory Diagnostic Decomposition & Placebo Controls
1. **Five-Part Portfolio-Path Decomposition:** Never evaluate an entry gate solely by reporting *"the skipped baseline trades had negative P&L."* Decompose total paired incremental RUB excess $\Delta X_{v,T} = X_{v,T} - X_{B,T}$ across **all calendar days** (including zero-trade days) into five exact components:
   - **(i)** Direct P&L and transaction friction avoided/forfeited on vetoed baseline entries;
   - **(ii)** P&L of subsequent entries newly enabled or displaced because vetoed trades freed finite RUB 30,000 capital *(paired with an uncapped-capital diagnostic run to separate signal selection from capital-slot reallocation)*;
   - **(iii)** Execution-delay, partial-leg de-risking, and transition-cost differences;
   - **(iv)** Net differences in retained LQDT/cash carry; and
   - **(v)** Differences in provisional risk-action (`RUB 5k / 10k / 15k`) trigger timing.
2. **No-Action Conditional Tagging:** Run `A2` executing 100% of baseline signals while tagging each trade ex ante with its decision-time regime state; if realized net P&L does not differ significantly across state tags, gating adds complexity without information.
3. **Persistence-Matched Random & Parity Placebos:** Benchmark every `A3`–`A5` challenger against **(a)** a block-preserving random entry-suppression gate matching the challenger's exact trade-veto fraction and state autocorrelation, and **(b)** two deterministic nonsense states (e.g., calendar day-of-month parity). Any challenger failing to clear placebo distributions is rejected as noise.
4. **Required Per-Rung Reporting Ledger:** Paired $\Delta X_{v,T}$ and absolute $(X_{v,T}, X_{B,T})$; excess-curve max drawdown and shortfall duration; **break-even per-contract cost** (friction level where $\Delta X_{v,T} = 0$); total and incremental round trips, gross turnover, rejected/stale/legged order counts, margin/collateral incidents, and human alert count; state occupancy %, transition count, and mean episode length.

### 11.2 Paired Episode-Clustered Uncertainty & Structural Sensitivity

Because ~10–15 liquid MOEX spread families react simultaneously to domestic rate decisions, sanctions, and session breaks, **individual spread legs and overlapping trades are never independent observations**:
- **Joint Calendar-Block / Stationary Bootstrap (`Politis & Romano 1994`):** Resample paired daily portfolio excess differences $d_{v,t} = \Delta X_{v,t} - \Delta X_{B,t}$ using **contiguous calendar blocks** (trading weeks, calendar months, or identified macro windows) **jointly across all instruments and challengers**, reporting 90% and 95% confidence intervals alongside block-length sensitivity. Do not mechanically resample across incompatible settlement breaks (`13.06.2024`, `23.03.2026`) without regime-era stratification.
- **Leave-One-Macro-Episode-Out & Leave-One-Family-Out:** Re-evaluate $\Delta X_{v,T}$ sequentially dropping **(a)** each major macro/policy episode (`Feb–Mar 2022` closure/20% rate shock, `June 2024` OFAC FX cessation & 2024 rate hikes, `2025–2026` rate/session transitions) and **(b)** each instrument family (`Si`/`CNY`, `BR`/`NG`, `MXI`/`MIX`/`RTS`, `GOLD`, single stocks).
- **Time-Varying Stability Check (`Giacomini & Rossi 2010`):** Compute fluctuation and one-time-reversal statistics on the cumulative relative-loss path to ensure a positive full-sample mean does not conceal a structural sign reversal in recent folds.

### 11.3 Multiple-Testing & Nested-Comparison Statistical Suite

While **Net RUB Excess over Matched LQDT ($\Delta X_{v,T}$)** remains the primary decision metric (never replaced by raw Sharpe ratios on sparse integer-lot portfolios), statistical survival requires reporting the joint search-adjusted battery across the **entire logged trial history (including all failed attempts)**:

1. **Nested-Model Predictive Accuracy:** **Clark & West (2007)** MSPE-adjusted statistic ($p < 0.05$) for nested regime-vs.-baseline comparisons, paired with one-sided Stationary Block Bootstrap test ($H_0: \mathbb{E}[d_{v,t}] \le 0$ rejected at $p < 0.025$).
2. **Model Confidence Set (`MCS` — Hansen, Lunde & Nason 2011):** Evaluated at the **90% confidence level** across `A0`–`A5`; to justify adoption, the regime challenger must reside **inside** $\widehat{\mathcal{M}}_{90\%}^*$ while the prior baseline rung (`A2`) is **excluded** (if both remain inside $\widehat{\mathcal{M}}_{90\%}^*$, parsimony mandates keeping `A2`).
3. **Specification-Search Familywise Control:** **White (2000) Reality Check**, **Hansen (2005) SPA**, or **Romano & Wolf (2005) StepM** stepdown bootstrap across all tested cards (`≤ 20` trial budget).
4. **Selection-Bias & Backtest-Overfitting Diagnostics:** Report **Deflated Sharpe Ratio (`DSR` $\ge 0.95$, Bailey & López de Prado 2014)** and **Probability of Backtest Overfitting (`PBO` $\le 0.20$, Bailey et al. 2017)** computed over the full configuration ledger, calibrated against the `MinBTL` table (§3.3) and **Harvey, Liu & Zhu's (2016)** $t > 3.0$ hurdle for novel predictive claims.

### 11.4 Sparse-State Minimums & Reconciled Economic Hurdles

- **Minimum State Sample Adequacy:** A candidate state must exhibit **occupancy $\ge 10\%$** of evaluation sessions, **$\ge 20$ non-overlapping state episodes**, and **$\ge 30$ non-overlapping affected decision/holding trade blocks distributed across $\ge 3$ separated historical periods** *(note: if evaluating high-turnover sub-variants, verify $\ge 30$ executed trades per 90d test block only where baseline cadence supports it, without penalizing selective zero-trade-day baselines into overtrading)*. States failing these counts are classified **`untestable / inconclusive — defer`**, never re-thresholded ex post.
- **Predeclared Net-RUB Economic Relevance Hurdle (Lock One Option Prior to Holdout Freeze):**
  - *Option E1 (Friction-Multiple Hurdle):* Incremental net RUB excess over the prior ladder rung $\Delta X_{v,T} \ge 2 \times \text{average round-trip trade friction}$ at Base Cost, with break-even per-contract cost strictly above Base Cost.
  - *Option E2 (Monitoring-Burden Floor):* $\Delta X_{v} \ge E^* = \max(\text{RUB } 1,000 \text{ per 90 calendar days},\; J)$, where $J$ prices incremental human monitoring/intervention burden ($\approx 1\times$ full 90-day LQDT carry of RUB 1,028).
  - *Option E3 (Strict Complexity Buffer):* $\Delta X_{v} \ge \text{RUB } 2,000 \text{ per 90-day evaluation block}$ ($\approx 2\times$ 90-day LQDT carry) required before permitting any multi-parameter or Stage-3 latent model.

## 12. Explicit Retain, Discard and Deferral Criteria and Post-Test Decision Matrix

### 12.1 Conjunctive Retain Gate
A regime-conditioning card is retained for Stage-3 / pilot consideration **if and only if ALL eight conditions hold simultaneously**:
1. **Verified Point-in-Time Provenance:** All state inputs, contract mappings, and rule editions are lawfully observable at $t^-$ and pass all 15 leakage checks (§10.3).
2. **Preregistered Single-Lever Isolation:** Mechanism, formula, threshold, sign, and single operational lever were frozen prior to evaluation within the $\le 20$-trial budget.
3. **Positive Economic Uplift on Untouched Holdout:** Paired $\Delta X_{v,T}$ exceeds the predeclared RUB hurdle ($E^*$) over the preceding ladder rung (`A2` for simple gates; best simple gate for Stage-3 challengers) **and** absolute $X_{v,T} > 0$ versus matched LQDT.
4. **Friction & Latency Survival:** $\Delta X_{v,T}$ preserves its positive sign across **Base**, **Conservative**, and **Stress** cost schedules and across **5-minute, 15-minute, and 90-minute** execution delays (break-even cost $>$ Base Cost).
5. **Fold, Episode & Family Stability:** Predeclared effect sign holds in $\ge 50\%\text{–}67\%$ of walk-forward folds (e.g., $\ge 2$ of $3$ folds), no single macro episode or single instrument family contributes $> 50\%$ of $\Delta X_{v,T}$, and Giacomini–Rossi tests show no recent structural reversal.
6. **Search-Adjusted Statistical Clearance:** Passes `MCS` (90%), `Clark–West` ($p < 0.05$) / paired block bootstrap ($p < 0.025$), beats persistence-matched random-gate null ($> 95\text{th}$ percentile), and satisfies `DSR` $\ge 0.95$ / `PBO` $\le 0.20$ with $\ge 30$ trade blocks across $\ge 3$ episodes.
7. **Risk & Capital Compliance:** Zero breaches of RUB 30,000 entry feasibility (zero emergency funding at entry), no silent core LQDT collateral usage, and does not hit RUB 5,000 warning or RUB 10,000 / 15,000 shortfall triggers faster than baseline.
8. **Operational Parsimony:** Incremental human alert/intervention load fits defined manual quotas and VPS memory/latency envelopes ($< 250\text{–}350\text{ MB}$ shared headroom if eventually deployed).

### 12.2 Exhaustive Immediate-Discard, Invalidation & Deferral Triggers

| Failure Category | Specific Trigger Condition | Mandatory Disposition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Data & Leakage** | Any look-ahead, full-sample smoothing/quantiles, edited-page backfill, survivorship bias, or unverified intraday quote assumption. | **Invalidate run immediately;** fix pipeline or **defer card** if point-in-time data do not exist. |
| **Baseline Prerequisite** | No-regime baseline has not been measured/validated, yields zero feasible RUB 30,000 integer entries, or both baseline and challenger lose heavily to LQDT. | **Halt regime testing;** resolve baseline feasibility or keep capital in LQDT. |
| **Cost & Delay Fragility** | $\Delta X_{v,T} \le 0$ at Base or Conservative cost; sign flips under Stress cost; uplift drops $\le 0$ when delay rises from $5\text{ min} \to 15\text{–}90\text{ min}$; break-even cost $<$ Base Cost; or fee drag exceeds **25%** of gross returns. | **Discard regime card permanently.** |
| **Fold & Concentration Fragility** | Underperforms baseline in $> 50\%$ of walk-forward folds; $> 50\%$ of incremental P&L comes from **one macro episode** (e.g., Feb 2022 or Jun 2024) or **one instrument family**; or untuned holdout fails hurdle. | **Discard / classify as fragile episode artifact** (never re-tune on holdout). |
| **Redundancy & Placebos** | Gains match random entry-suppression placebo, replicate a simple baseline threshold tweak, coincide purely with expiry/closure calendars, or require changing sizing/exits/pairs simultaneously with entry. | **Discard regime label;** retain simpler unconditional threshold or deterministic `R3` calendar rule. |
| **Sizing & State Whipsaw** | Integer-lot rounding ($w_t$) truncates $> 50\%$ of signals below 1 contract; excluded pairs held $> 50\%$ of baseline profit; latent model switches $> 3\text{ times/day}$; or HMM refit label-flip rate $> 20\%$. | **Discard sizing / latent model.** |
| **Risk & Sample Limits** | Hits RUB 5k warning faster or breaches RUB 10k / 15k limits while baseline stays safe; occupancy $< 10\%$, $< 20$ state episodes, or $< 30$ trade blocks; `DSR` $< 0.95$ / `PBO` $> 0.20$; trial count $> 20$ without `MinBTL` support; or **30 calendar days** of Stage-2 work elapse without a card passing all gates. | **Close R13 as Inconclusive / Deferred;** revert strictly to `A2` (no-regime baseline + `R3` feasibility gates). |

### 12.3 Post-Test Architectural Scenarios

- **Scenario A — Observable Extreme-Volatility Gate (`G1`/`G2`) Survives:** Adopt strictly as a low-parameter **risk/slippage entry throttle (`R2`)**—verifying whether gains show a clear mechanistic signature concentrated in sessions with discrete auctions, price-corridor hits, or settlement-price gaps rather than spurious return timing. Test `L1-HMM` only if a concrete dynamic lag remains unresolved or once $\ge 3$ homogeneous post-`14.09.2026` years accumulate to satisfy `MinBTL`.
- **Scenario B — Only Liquidity (`F1-Liq`/`G4-Turn`) or Policy-Calendar (`F3-Cal`) Survives:** Permanently reclassify the winning filter as **execution-eligibility / dated policy-calendar infrastructure (`R3`)** inside the baseline; discard volatility, carry, and latent regime layers.
- **Scenario C — No Gate Survives Ablation or Sample Is Underpowered:** Log R13 formally as *"no incremental out-of-sample net RUB evidence; regime conditioning deferred,"* reallocate engineering focus to baseline execution quality, broker fee/GO verification, and position sizing, and revisit regimes only if multi-year post-`14.09.2026` capital/data scale (`RUB 10–20M`) warrants a fresh preregistered study.
