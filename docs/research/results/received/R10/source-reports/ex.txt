**Common design and accounting:** Register in advance a finite pair universe by underlying and expiry adjacency, with minimum contemporaneous two-leg volume/OI and quote-quality gates set on TRAINING ONLY. Every day t use only data actually available before the decision timestamp, not the day's final OHLC if entering before close. EOD signal at t implies earliest permissible execution after publication (normally next eligible session); replay 3/5/15/30/90-minute reaction cases and a measured distribution once available. A daily bar cannot reveal contemporaneous two-leg depth, crossing sequence or partial fills: penalize/suppress fills conservatively until lawful snapshots/records exist. Archive exact dated security identifiers, multiplier m in RUB per quoted price unit (tick value RUB/tick size in quoted units, with any currency-conversion convention), near/far expiries, fixing and last-trade rules, board and session calendars, holiday calendars, trading status, revised tariffs, OI publication time and margin schedule; do not splice ‘continuous’ contracts by hindsight. Normalize equal-underlying calendar as b_t=ln(F_far,t/F_near,t)*365/(T_far−T_near), only for strictly positive prices, same quote units, valid settlement dates and matched snapshots; otherwise use signed RUB pair value S_t=m_far F_far,t−h m_near F_near,t and RUB-risk-normalized residual Z_t=(S_t−train-only conditional fair value)/train-only robust scale (MAD or winsorized volatility). Log-spread is a signal, not the actual RUB P&L. For unlike underlyings, train-only robust beta/hedge fit on overlapping RUB price changes, freeze per evaluation fold, and reject if unstable; compare simple economic 1:1 matched exposures before any fitted hedge. Integer legs solve a small bounded minimization of RUB exposure mismatch and margin subject to exact lot integers, gross exposure and cash/VM constraints; compute P&L from actual tick/multiplier/FX rules, fees, partial fills and settlement, never from fractional theoretical weights. Preregister shared grids, e.g. training lookback 60/120/250 eligible days; normalized entry |Z| ≥1.5/2/2.5; target |Z| ≤0.25/0.5; hard time stop 10/20/40 trading days, plus expiry buffer 3/5 eligible sessions; test FAR fewer combinations than the Cartesian product by staged screening and disclose every tried combination. Values are candidate hypotheses, NOT calibrated rules or risk bounds. Fit hedge, seasonal curve, scales, ranks, size and decision thresholds only on training; chronological rolling/expanding walk-forward, embargo at least maximal signal lookback dependence as appropriate and actual overlapping hold/roll horizon around fold boundaries, evaluate all eligible opportunities and failures, then one final untouched contiguous holdout. Data revisions/corrected candles and missing expired contracts are missingness tests, not gaps to forward-fill. Family-wise candidate log, multiplicity-aware uncertainty (e.g. fold dispersion/block bootstrap), placebo direction and shifted-signal checks, stress across regimes, and minimum effective sample and exposure diversity gate. No statistically significant correlation, raw price z-score or cointegration p-value alone implies funding-based convergence, bounded stopping time, executable two-leg fills or net positive RUB excess.
