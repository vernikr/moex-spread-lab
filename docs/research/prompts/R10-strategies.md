## Investigate
1. Propose a compact family of simple economic/statistical baselines for distinct spread classes, e.g. justified carry/basis/calendar relationships and robust spread mean-reversion hypotheses. Explain why raw z-scores/correlation/stationarity tests alone are not an economic mechanism or a proof of tradeability.

5. Opportunity-arrival modeling must be based only on past available opportunities, including stale/rejected ideas. Avoid an oracle that always reallocates to the ex-post best trade. Test whether a simpler ranked queue achieves similar results.
