# R06 — MOEX futures universe and defensible spread relationships

You are researching one decision for a private MOEX futures-spread project. Produce an evidence-backed report, not software or investment recommendations. Work in English.

The user is a qualified investor and reports KPUR status; broker-specific recognition and consequences are unverified.
- Test allocation: RUB 30,000 including margin, variation-margin liquidity and reserves, on the SAME account as a core LQDT holding exceeding RUB 40 million. Future candidate scale: RUB 10–20 million. Internal budgets do not ring-fence the account or prove broker collateral eligibility.

Continuing LQDT return on eligible retained collateral must not be deducted twice. Exclude loss of the LQDT long-term holding deduction only; investor tax residence/account treatment is unknown.
- User accepts RUB 10,000–15,000 cumulative underperformance versus LQDT over several months. Delegated, provisional assistant defaults: warning at RUB 5,000 excess-curve drawdown; action at RUB 10,000 drawdown from the excess-result high-water mark or cumulative LQDT shortfall of RUB 10,000; outer shortfall boundary RUB 15,000; formal review at 90 calendar days without resetting losses. At the 10k cumulative-shortfall trigger, any funding is solely to reach flat. Entry must pass with zero emergency funding. Core collateral must not be silently treated as test funds.
- Manual reaction is 3–90 minutes, typical median approximately 5–15 minutes. Distribution unknown. One realistic virtual portfolio attempts every eligible signal without discretionary omission, but records stale/rejected/unfilled/partially filled outcomes and respects finite capital. Human trade/intervention quota and emergency response availability still need definition. Monitoring 24/7 does not mean all contracts trade 24/7.
- Free data/resources preferred; no foreign cards/phones, small justified one-off spending may be considered. Finest FREE defensible timeframe is a research question, not an assumed one-minute/live entitlement. Dark-only future screener and Telegram alerts. MacBook Pro Intel 6-core i7, 16 GB RAM, Radeon Pro 5300M 4 GB. VPS: 1 vCPU, 1 GB RAM + 2 GB swap, 20 GB disk, active VPN and other workload; 250–350 MB background budget is shared and unverified. ## Question
Which current AND historical futures families have an economic reason to form a medium-term spread, and how should candidates be excluded before statistical searching? Do not deliver an unexplained matrix of all correlated price series.

## Investigate
1. Date-stamped current/expired contract inventory and official specifications: underlying, expiration/delivery, multiplier, tick and ruble tick value, quotation/settlement currency, settlement methodology, limits, sessions and liquidity indicators. Include perpetual-style contracts ONLY if verified; explain distinct funding/settlement economics and do not treat every ticker as a conventional dated future.
2. Candidate classes: same-underlying calendars; related benchmarks; equity/index links; commodity/FX-related pairs or conversions only where tradeable and economically justified. Distinguish exact convergence, basis/carry relationships and purely statistical mean reversion; list break scenarios and necessary auxiliary inputs.
3. Hedge ratios in units of economic exposure and integer contracts, not raw price correlation; asymmetric multipliers, cross-currency FX conversion and conversion-rate timing. Handle changing specifications and delivery/expiry mismatch. Two futures may require missing cash/FX exposure to complete a purported arbitrage.
4. Liquidity overlap across both legs, holding horizon, event/dividend/funding exposure, realistic entry/exit capacity and minimum position feasibility. Current free-float/volume should not be applied retroactively to old universes.
5. Screening that covers a broad inventory but restricts pair generation to defendable relationships; explain how this reduces false discoveries and resource cost without preselecting only today's survivors.
