# Unified execution proof (`/`, `/proof`)

The local workbench evaluates ordinary pairs and native calendar spreads on cached historical price proxies. It never reconstructs executable fills, reconciles a broker account or models a shared-capital basket: prices are candle/settlement proxies (A-05/A-06/A-08), and there is no broker connection or executable-return claim.

## Homepage, automatic parameters and stability lamps (2026-11-18)

`/` and `/proof` serve the same screen and deep links.

### Scope and compatibility

- Click a name to inspect its chart; check its box to include it in strategy search. Checks survive switching assets; asset/family boxes cascade with a mixed state for partial subtrees. Upper **Все** controls the catalog, lower **Все** the open asset. No checks means exactly the open window; an unchecked focus is never silently added. The first check initializes a shared parameter editor whose vector and strategy stay pinned while browsing; clearing all checks restores the per-leaf editor.
- Search chooses **one common strategy and one common vector** for all checked windows, never per-window winners. `mean_reversion.lookback` maps to calendar `zscore_mean_reversion.window`; thresholds share intersecting stepped domains. Pair and calendar MR share next-open (default), next-close and explicitly diagnostic same-bar close fills; carry and RUONIA do not cross universes. Carry participates under its own compulsory `same_day_settlement` even with a delayed or same-bar reference fill. Size and slippage are never search axes.
- `POST /api/proof/optimize` accepts the focused request with `optimize_strategy: true` plus optional `targets` (unique `{universe, pair}` / `{universe, asset, pair}`); an empty array uses the focus. Preflight every candidate family on the fixed cohort, skipping an incompatible family, not its valid windows. Only a window rejected by every applicable family may be proposed for removal: **before** changing checks, `selection_requires_confirmation` returns exclusions, reasons and the remaining count with zero trials. Disjoint incompatibilities return `no_common_strategy`; Cancel/Escape leaves the selection unchanged, and a trial-level failure does **not** authorize removing a window.
- **Research screens (settings dialog).** `allow_failed_volume` and `allow_failed_capital` default to `true` independently; checked = allow the respective failed check. They govern proof search, live stability probes and the matching proof/evaluation only — never additional windows, raw V/C verdicts, S2/CLI gates or trading authority.

### Selection, accounting and evidence

- [BR-10](../../../docs/stages/04-requirements.md): **Настройки → Доля истории для проверки** is one persistent 0–50% preference, default 0 (`GET/POST /api/settings`; ignored `data/analytics/research-settings.json`). Old split-only files keep their share with default permissions, one slippage tick and RUB 30,000 capital, without a read-time rewrite. Snapshot all preferences once per request and split **each window's own eligible sessions**: at 0% score full training, otherwise reserve the last `max(1, floor(n × percent / 100))` sessions; journal-only rows do not consume the share.
- Each window starts an independent account with the global `initial_capital_rub` (default RUB 30,000): free cash sits in LQDT, posted margin earns nothing until released, ledger P&L includes costs and funding, and the benchmark is fully invested in LQDT. For `d` elapsed days, score `100 × [(V_end/V_start)^(365/d) − (LQDT_end/LQDT_start)^(365/d)]` pp/yr; positive sub-year spans are annualized **projections**.
- The objective is the **arithmetic mean** of annualized excesses over the fixed cohort; an idle LQDT-only book contributes zero with full weight, and missing/unmeasurable training prunes the proposal rather than scoring zero. The former 10k/15k LQDT-relative limits **no longer veto**: a book behind LQDT stays admissible and is scored by its measured excess, risk path visible.
- Run Optuna **TPESampler** independently per compatible family: 8 startup trials, **80 proposals per family**, seeded from the global **Сид** (`random_seed`): non-zero is used verbatim and reproduces the run; `0` draws one fresh base seed per run, each family deriving its own child seed. Integer windows, 0.1 threshold steps, `exit_z < entry_z`; apply the maximum admissible tested proposal, preferring the current vector on an in-family exact tie, then the first tied proposal, then the current family, then declared order.
- **Only after selection**, measure the rule on each continuous full ledger; validation begins at the last training mark without resetting cash, positions, costs or funding. The group result shows members, their rates, the equal-weight training mean and the labelled result-period mean; a missing validation rate leaves the mean unavailable.
- **Lamps are diagnostics, not vetoes.** Probe ±1/±2 feasible input steps, training only; `100 × clamp(worst neighbor / center, 0, 1)`, green ≥70, amber ≥40. With checks, lamps profile the common vector by averaging member annualized excesses at each point. Absent exposure, <24 training sessions or unavailable evidence leaves a lamp unscored; probes are not Optuna proposals.
- `POST /api/proof/plateau` probes an explicit strategy/vector on `targets` without Optuna, trial logs or a focus instrument; navigation replays only the inspected chart and cannot restart group lamps.
- `proof-search.v4` records family identity/fill per proposal, `selected_strategy` / `selected_fill`, fixed targets, per-family budget/status/best scores, rank score, member scores in canonical order (or pruning reason) and dated evaluation metadata in ignored `data/analytics/proof-searches.jsonl`. Calendar data and member snapshots are reused per request; optimize/checked-plateau requests permit 192 KiB and ≤1024 targets, the window table 192 KiB and ≤128 requests. Calls omitting `optimize_strategy` retain the 40-trial active-family `proof-search.v3` path; `price_fill` records the fill convention for a carry draft and is never searched.

## Window table, signal lines and reference fills (2026-11-18)

- The lower selector shows ticker-free **Период / V / C / Сд. / п.п./г** rows sorted by actual end/start dates descending; periods are one-line `yyyy.mm-yyyy.mm` with full dates in the title. V/C keep the original one-spread verdicts with focusable icons and tooltips; a blue download arrow marks missing local data, not a failed screen.
- **Сд.** counts accepted entries across the whole current simulation, including open-at-end positions. **п.п./г** is the exact evaluation's annualized excess over LQDT: validation when enabled, otherwise training. A genuine zero stays zero; pending/unmeasurable results are dashes with reasons. Each row is an independent account, never the cohort mean or a stale search winner.
- `POST /api/proof/windows` reuses `StudyWindow.report(seed)` without optimization, mutation or preference writes; it snapshots settings/benchmark once and returns per-row counts, evaluation, selection and run ID, not full ledgers. Invalid data/parameters fail that row; identity/list/duplicate errors reject the request.
- The raw spread pane starts **expanded**, independent of the normalization selector. Thin buy/sell curves are the strategy's **entry thresholds at the signal bar's close** — not bands, exit rules or fill prices. `buy_level` / `sell_level` use raw price units; `_rel` fields divide by that signal date's far-leg value ×100. Warm-up, zero-volume and zero-deviation rows have no bands.
- Pair MR uses the engine's previous-window population deviation; native MR uses sample deviation in RUB, excluding the current bar for delayed fills but including it in same-bar reference mode, converted by that date's RUB point value including FX. RUONIA boundaries invert `dev_pp` at ±`threshold_pp`, using `near_price × 91 / 365 / 100` raw points per rate percentage point. Transforms never feed thresholds or strategies.
- `next_open` = one eligible-bar delay/open, `next_close` = one eligible-bar delay/close, `reference_same_bar` = zero-delay/close. The ordinary-pair adapter and common cohort forward all three, including CNYRUBF and GLDRUBF; zero-delay analytics stay restricted to `diagnostic_only` close fills. Same-bar signals already use the close, so they are not achievable post-signal fills.
- `execution_reference` marks proof/selection/evaluation, group summaries and live-profile metadata; tooltips, summaries and CSV warn these are **reference results, not executable-return evidence**.

## Automatic per-entry size and global slippage (2026-11-18)

- The workbench sends `sizing=max_affordable`, never manual contracts. `position_sizing.py` replays unit **signal intents** through one funded account per window. Before each entry take the largest whole number of spreads within `max(0, min(initial_capital_rub, last observable wealth + an earlier same-row exit P&L − exit costs))`; fix the quantity until exit. Losses shrink the next trade; profits never expand the original authority; there are no top-ups, collateral or former 50-contract ceilings. An unaffordable intent is an explained skip with zero exposure, retried only at the next declared entry.
- Affordability covers actual margin and rounded entry costs **plus one exit side at entry prices** — never eventual exit prices or full-window maximum margin. Pair margin uses the validated two-leg snapshot; native-calendar margin uses fill-leg prices and the reference margin rate. Missing/nonpositive/nonfinite terms are errors, not unlimited financing.
- The capital stress screen preserves the engine conventions: pair `3×M×q + 5000×q + 5000`, native calendar `(3×M + 5000 + 5000)×q`, plus both cost sides. Allowing a failed screen removes those stress reserves, **not** ordinary affordability; raw V/C flags still describe the one-spread window screen, not a claim that maximum size is safe at the observed volume.
- Actual fees/funding, posted margin, free cash and wealth drive replay and scoring; funding belongs only to accepted held exposure. New entries pause on **negative free cash only** — the former 10k/15k pause is removed — and historical funding shortfalls invalidate scoring, never inject money.
- **Настройки → Проскальзывание** is the only slippage control: 0–10 ticks, default 1, persisted as `slippage_ticks`; it applies to every strategy/window — once per side for a native spread, per leg/side for a pair. Changes invalidate proofs, searches and lamps and notify other tabs; slippage is never optimized or sensitivity-varied.
- `selection.quantity=null` with `selection.sizing=max_affordable` identifies the policy; native strategy parameters stay separate from fixed `strategy_conventions`. Journal badges, tooltips and CSV (`contracts_held`, `entry_contracts`, `exit_contracts`) show actual sizes. Calls omitting `sizing` retain the fixed-size policy; direct API/CLI defaults and archived studies stay unchanged.

## Global capital and quieter results (2026-11-18)

- **Настройки → Капитал · ₽** is one project-wide research assumption, `initial_capital_rub`, default **30,000**, stored with the split/screens/slippage. The UI/API accept RUB **0.01–1,000,000,000** with at most two decimals; the bound is an implementation guard, not funding approval. Invalid types and out-of-range values are rejected without replacing the file; old files get the default.
- One immutable settings snapshot drives all members/trials/probes/replays of a request; the same per-window setting is **not** a shared-basket allocation. Changing capital invalidates proofs, window readings, profiles and searches, recalculates quantities, V/C and margin percentages, preserves the checked cohort/strategy/execution and notifies other tabs; request keys and version guards discard late old-budget responses. Neither capital nor fill is an Optuna dimension.
- This changes a **research hypothesis** only: no pilot funding, emergency-money, fixed RUB 5k/10k/15k boundary or broker-authority relaxation, and no automatic top-up or loss relaxation.

## Global random seed and removed benchmark-relative limits (2026-11-20)

- **Настройки → Сид случайности** is one project-wide preference, `random_seed`, default **0**, stored with the other settings. The UI/API accept an integer **0–2,147,483,647**; other types and out-of-range values are rejected without replacing the file; old files get the default.
- The seed is the base for **every** random choice in the project, today the Optuna TPESampler proposals: non-zero reproduces the run; `0` draws one fresh base seed per run, so repeated runs may differ while one run stays internally consistent. The drawn value is reported as `seed_used` beside `seed`, so any run can be replayed by pinning it.
- The **10k excess-drawdown and 15k shortfall limits relative to LQDT no longer reject a strategy**: not in proof search, not in the retired S2 grid, and no longer pausing replay entries. A candidate behind LQDT stays admissible and is scored by its measured excess; drawdown, shortfall and the free-cash path stay visible. Only **negative free cash** stops new entries; a cash shortfall still invalidates scoring and never injects money.
- These are **research-hypothesis changes only**: no change to pilot funding, emergency-money rules, fixed RUB 5k/10k/15k boundaries, broker authority or any executable-return statement.

## Signed funding in mean reversion (2026-11-17)

For `perpetual_quarterly_same_asset`, `mean_reversion` joins local MOEX ISS FORTS EOD history on trade date and requires a published, verified or explicitly estimated rate for each clearing; the cash effect per clearing is `−position × SWAPRATE × near_lot × quantity`, feeding ledger P&L, equity/LQDT comparison and trade gross/net. On next-open proxies, funding counts only while the position stays open through that day's proxy clearing. The pair screener requires a local near-perpetual funding-history cache: an unresolved gap prevents the proof and missing values are never silently zero. Non-perpetual calendars receive no funding. MOEX EOD `SWAPRATE` is the exchange-published clearing result; the app never reconstructs minute-average `D` or `K1/K2` regimes, and the lot comes from the contracts intake snapshot.

### Exchange check and short gaps (2026-11-18)

- One offline resolver serves the pair catalogue, `mean_reversion`, `funding_only`, training replays and the S2 pair screener. A finite published rate, **including zero**, wins; a supplemental MOEX re-download can supply a missing number. A documented extra session without clearing has **no payment and no rate**: keep genuine candles in price replay, never add a weekend settlement to carry, never move or duplicate the next regular rate; without a same-day LQDT quote use only its last observed close and identify its date.
- For **1–3 consecutive missing clearing sessions** only, book the **same** signed `(previous + next) / 2` per underlying unit on every missing date. Resolve on the perpetual's complete local EOD history **before** selecting a quarterly or its positive-volume dates. Never bridge 4+ sessions, an unbounded edge, an unverified clearing, the 2022 FX regime change, the sample end or the training/validation boundary; no midpoint restores a missing `SETTLEPRICE`.
- Cash is attributed retrospectively under the existing position/sign/lot rules. A carry decision needs a complete prior window: a midpoint is available **after** its right-hand clearing, never for the same settlement decision. A prefix or training account that would need a later rate is unavailable, not computed with validation data. Ledger/CSV distinguish a published zero, a flat position, `rechecked`, `midpoint` (both source dates/rates) and `no_clearing`. See [historical intake](../../data/docs/historical-pair-intake.md#funding-reconciliation-2026-11-18) for live-source evidence and the read-only recheck command.

## Spread display & causal normalization (2026-11-16)

- Series carry `spread_rel` / `spread_open_rel` / `spread_last_trade_rel`: the spread as a percentage of the far leg (pair: RUB-normalized near−far over far contract value; calendar: points over points) — a pure same-row ratio with no look-ahead. Per-fill blocks carry `spread_fill_rel`; the spread pane plots the relative spread.
- The normalization select (`#norm`, spread chart only) offers raw, residual vs expanding mean, residual vs trailing rolling mean (20/60), rolling z-score (20) and residual vs trailing OLS detrend (60). Every transform is strictly causal and a visual scale only — it never feeds signals, and markers stay tied to trades.
- Table spread cells show % of far with the absolute value secondary; CSV gains `spread_rel` / `spread_open_rel` / `spread_last_trade_rel` (absolute columns kept). P&L, ledger, tiles and totals stay in RUB. `run_id` changes versus slice-3 payloads for identical selections; `execution-proof.v1` remains a strict superset — additive optional fields only.

## Reconciliation contract (asserted in tests)

- `Σ ledger.costs == Σ trade entry+exit costs` (±0.02 ₽ rounding).
- `Σ ledger.mark_pnl == Σ trade gross` (telescoping; pair-book prices are RUB-normalized `near*mv − far*mv`).
- `last(ledger.cumulative_net) == Σ trade net` (±0.02 ₽); `ledger_reconciliation_rub == 0.0`.
- `cost_totals` equals the sum of per-side component dictionaries; the broker component is always 0.0 (VTB zero fee to 2026-12-31). Same inputs → identical `run_id`.

## Runtime performance contracts

Cached, native and parallel paths must return results **bit-for-bit identical** to the single-threaded reference (`MOEX_PROOF_REFERENCE_EVAL=1`, `MOEX_PROOF_DISABLE_C=1`, `MOEX_PROOF_WORKERS=1`), preserving admission, sizing, funding, cost and risk semantics, IEEE-754 arithmetic without FMA contraction, the Optuna trajectory, `rank_score`, `plateau` lamps, `canonical reason` and `run_id`. Gates: `packages/proof/tests/test_perf_equivalence.py` and `packages/proof/tests/test_fastcore_parity.py` — a change that cannot preserve them is a contract decision, not a refactor.

- **Input caches.** Parsed JSON and SHA-256 are LRU-cached by `(resolved_path, st_mtime_ns, st_size)` (`_HashedJSONCache`, `core/w0.py`), as are intake artifacts, EOD histories, LQDT series, window slices/`window_meta`, rolling z-values, prefix sums, H1 windows and sealed-table hashes; the key invalidates instantly on in-place edits, `StudyWindow.full` is a lazy `cached_property` built once, and `MOEX_OFFLINE_ONLY=1` blocks network re-downloads.
- **`_fastcore` (C99).** Hot numeric loops live in `packages/core/src/moex_spread_lab/core/_fastcore.c`, compiled lazily with `clang`/`cc -O3 -ffp-contract=off -shared -fPIC` under a lock and atomically swapped in; without a compiler or with `MOEX_PROOF_DISABLE_C=1` it falls back to the equivalent pure-Python implementation. It provides zero-copy `Py_buffer` primitives over `array` window columns, `compute_rolling_z`, the native calendar engines (`calendar_sample_z` double-double arithmetic, `calendar_zscore_scan`, `calendar_convergence_scan`), `clean_obj`, `mean_reversion_scan` and `size_proof_metrics` with `exact_round2` and the `scalar_only` training mode.
- **`metrics_only` pipeline.** Training trials and plateau probes skip presentation-only work (commissions, LQDT overlay, notes, `_rel_pct` series, JSON serialization, `summary` assembly) while finalizing every structural quantity the metric depends on; `size_proof` runs without `deepcopy(unit)` and builds a compact 4-field ledger.
- **`CohortWorkerPool`.** Batch work (`batch_search`, `strategy_search`, `checked_plateau`, `window_summaries`) uses a long-lived `spawn` pool: one `ProcessPoolExecutor(max_workers=1)` per slot, activated with ≥3 valid windows and ≥1500 bars (or `MOEX_PROOF_FORCE_POOL=1`), bounded by `MOEX_PROOF_WORKERS`. Windows are LPT-assigned over a cached sealed manifest, ordered canonically per shard; `StudyWindow` instances are built once per worker and kept in `_WORKER_COHORTS`. Evaluation uses adaptive one-jump fan-out with a two-phase Canary + fan-out on failure that guarantees the canonical minimum-index rejection reason; cancellation is polled cooperatively (`POLL_INTERVAL_SECONDS = 0.05`) with cohort release in `finally`. Monkeypatched test components fall back to the single-process path.
- **Progress streaming.** With `X-Progress-Stream: 1`, the proof endpoints emit `application/x-ndjson` progress events while keeping JSON compatibility; the UI separates request channels and cancels stale requests via `AbortController`.

## Registered funding-carry strategies

A distinct pair-only, daily strategy may opt into `carry_settlement_intents.v1` with `same_day_settlement` and a non-empty, entirely tunable numeric schema. The proof boundary validates event chronology, pair/volume/expiry and assignment constraints and independently computes funding, settlement cash flows, fees, benchmark and sizing from evidence; plugin-supplied money is discarded. Forced terminal marks are not executable exits, and normal exits lose exit-day funding. Installed code is trusted: strategy conformance and causal-truncation tests remain mandatory. See `packages/strategies/docs/carry-intent-boundary.md`.
