Reserve worst-case funds for overlapping live orders; do not release on a cancellation request alone. For two independent legs, compare leg-first alternatives using immediately executable depth, expected unhedged stress and liquidity; never assert universal liquid-leg-first. Set maximum unhedged duration, maximum allowed adverse hedge price and fallback flatten action in advance. On unexpected partial first fill, hedge only the feasible matched ratio; cancel remainder or de-risk residual subject to real acknowledgments. If second leg has no quote or violates bound, do not fabricate a hedge; record a naked exposure incident and conservative exit path. Even flattening can slip or fail.
