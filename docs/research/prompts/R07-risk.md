## Question
Are the delegated pilot defaults internally coherent and feasible for integer futures spreads with 3–90 minute reaction, and what bounded sizing/risk rules should be tested? The defaults are not targets to validate at any cost. No-trade is a valid result.

## Investigate
1. Separate relative economic loss, absolute P&L, peak drawdown, cash/VM path, broker margin/collateral, gross exposure, temporary single-leg risk and account-wide recourse. Existing margin offsets may be removed. Emergency replenishment is not assumed timely or authorized at entry.
2. Evaluate initial 30k all-in allocation, 15k lifetime gross additional-funding cap (45k gross total), 5k warning, 10k drawdown/shortfall action, 15k outer LQDT shortfall boundary. The 5k gap between action and outer boundary is NOT proved enough. Require a positive uncertainty buffer in addition to stressed post-signal/post-trigger loss and exit costs; test joint price/margin/liquidity shocks, not additive independent best cases.
3. At entry and every reliable review, test `X - stressed incremental loss - positive uncertainty buffer >= -15,000`, or a tighter justified rule, and define feasible entry/continuation inequalities for remaining risk budget and already available liquidity across the delay/exit window. Avoid counting liquidation costs twice if included in X. Benchmark-return accrual, changing prices and negative VM can consume different budgets at different times. If no minimum-lot spread fits, reject it.
4. Test, do not assume, a 50%-of-initial-funds ceiling on starting margin and a 5k stress-buffer heuristic. Replace them if evidence supports tighter rules; don't prescribe them universally. Margin stress multipliers and return tails need sourced historical/scenario grounding; no Gaussian-tail guarantee.
5. Compare fixed minimum contracts, bounded risk-scaled sizing, capped fractional-Kelly-like methods ONLY if probabilities/calibration support them, scale-ins with precommitted aggregate caps, and low/medium/high risk profiles. Include correlated concurrent trades and discrete rounding. The high-risk research profile cannot enlarge this pilot's authority.
6. Examine future 10–20m capacity, funding concentration and joint impact. Define stable promotion gates; tiny pilot profit alone cannot establish capacity or tail safety.
