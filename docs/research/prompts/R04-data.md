# R04 — Free market data, fine intervals and point-in-time coverage

You are researching one decision for a private MOEX futures-spread project. Produce an evidence-backed report, not software or investment recommendations. Work in English.

## Standalone project context
- Selective medium-term futures spread ideas across an economically defensible broad universe; zero-trade days are acceptable. Initial execution is manual at VTB, later automation potentially at BCS. The user is a qualified investor and reports KPUR status; broker-specific recognition and consequences are unverified.
- Test allocation: RUB 30,000 including margin, variation-margin liquidity and reserves, on the SAME account as a core LQDT holding exceeding RUB 40 million. Future candidate scale: RUB 10–20 million. Internal budgets do not ring-fence the account or prove broker collateral eligibility.
- Every performance measure must address a matched LQDT counterfactual with identical true external flows. Core LQDT diverted for the pilot remains invested in the counterfactual. Continuing LQDT return on eligible retained collateral must not be deducted twice. Exclude loss of the LQDT long-term holding deduction only; investor tax residence/account treatment is unknown.
- User accepts RUB 10,000–15,000 cumulative underperformance versus LQDT over several months. Delegated, provisional assistant defaults: warning at RUB 5,000 excess-curve drawdown; action at RUB 10,000 drawdown from the excess-result high-water mark or cumulative LQDT shortfall of RUB 10,000; outer shortfall boundary RUB 15,000; formal review at 90 calendar days without resetting losses. These are unvalidated action rules, not guaranteed bounds.
- Emergency additional allocation: at most RUB 15,000 cumulative GROSS for the whole pilot, making gross contributed funding at most RUB 45,000. Every use needs explicit approval and may only avert disorderly liquidation/complete de-risking of existing positions, never fund fresh exposure/averaging down or reset losses/the lifetime allowance. At the 10k cumulative-shortfall trigger, any funding is solely to reach flat. Entry must pass with zero emergency funding. Broker-initiated use of core collateral counts toward the gross allowance and is an incident if unapproved; sale proceeds are not automatically economic loss in X. Core collateral must not be silently treated as test funds.
- Manual reaction is 3–90 minutes, typical median approximately 5–15 minutes. Distribution unknown. One realistic virtual portfolio attempts every eligible signal without discretionary omission, but records stale/rejected/unfilled/partially filled outcomes and respects finite capital. Human trade/intervention quota and emergency response availability still need definition. Monitoring 24/7 does not mean all contracts trade 24/7.
- Free data/resources preferred; no foreign cards/phones, small justified one-off spending may be considered. Finest FREE defensible timeframe is a research question, not an assumed one-minute/live entitlement. Dark-only future screener and Telegram alerts. MacBook Pro Intel 6-core i7, 16 GB RAM, Radeon Pro 5300M 4 GB. VPS: 1 vCPU, 1 GB RAM + 2 GB swap, 20 GB disk, active VPN and other workload; 250–350 MB background budget is shared and unverified. No production probing/deployment now.
- Stage 2 consists of prompts; the user will execute research. Do not claim backtests, account checks, legal verification or performance measurements you did not actually perform. Technology choices, implementation and real trading are later gates.

## Evidence and response requirements
Use the actual research date as the current-facts cutoff (pack prepared 2026-10-07). Use current/historical primary documents with applicable dates; distinguish publication, availability, effective time and access time. Cite every material factual claim with URL/issuer/title/page or section/version/date and a short supporting excerpt; books/papers need bibliographic locations. Mark assumptions and unknowns explicitly; absent information is not zero cost or permission.
Return: (1) decision summary with go/conditional/no-go, (2) claim/evidence table using this research ID as prefix, (3) the task-specific tables/calculations/protocols below, (4) conflicts and gaps with exact questions for the owner/broker, (5) falsification/acceptance criteria and uncertainty, (6) proposed project changes and refresh triggers. State which inputs were actually read and which requested checks were only proposed. Use public, permitted, read-only examples if useful; no credentials, live trades, paid subscriptions, big downloads or production access. Missing dependency reports make relevant conclusions conditional; they do not prevent independent evidence collection.

## Question
What is the finest practically useful FREE MOEX futures data that can support this project, historically and in live monitoring, and which conclusions are impossible without paid/collected quote data?

## Investigate
1. Inventory official MOEX ISS and other official publicly accessible services, broker feeds and lawful free alternatives. Verify current terms, auth, Russian card/phone/payment dependencies, personal-use/storage/redistribution rights, latency, throttling, bulk access and retention. A public endpoint is not evidence of unlimited rights or real-time data.
2. Per instrument family, inspect coverage of expired/current contracts, candles at offered intervals, trades, bid/ask, depth, volume/open interest, settlement/clearing prices, price limits, margin/risk parameters and changes, specifications/multipliers, calendars, currencies and event/reference data. Distinguish missing, paid and not retained historically.
3. List supported intervals AND actual available history depth, earliest/latest timestamps, pagination/time-zone semantics, corrections, trading-day boundaries, missing sessions and survivorship. Check contracts from multiple periods, including a delisted/expired example where feasible; do not claim complete coverage from one successful request.
4. Time semantics: event vs publication vs receipt, market delays, asynchronous leg timestamps, stale bars, weekend schedules, settlement marks vs executable quotes, index/FX conversion availability. Do not forward-fill through closures into fictional tradability.
5. Data sizing/CPU/network costs for universe and pair classes. Estimate with explicit record count and row-size assumptions; distinguish measured from calculated. Retention/aggregation alternatives should follow measurement, not silently abandon fine intervals.
