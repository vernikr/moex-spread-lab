You are researching one decision for a private MOEX futures-spread project. Produce an evidence-backed report, not software or investment recommendations. Work in English.

- Test allocation: RUB 30,000 including margin, variation-margin liquidity and reserves, on the SAME account as a core LQDT holding exceeding RUB 40 million. Future candidate scale: RUB 10–20 million. Internal budgets do not ring-fence the account or prove broker collateral eligibility.
- Every performance measure must address a matched LQDT counterfactual with identical true external flows. Core LQDT diverted for the pilot remains invested in the counterfactual. Continuing LQDT return on eligible retained collateral must not be deducted twice. Exclude loss of the LQDT long-term holding deduction only; investor tax residence/account treatment is unknown.

One realistic virtual portfolio attempts every eligible signal without discretionary omission, but records stale/rejected/unfilled/partially filled outcomes and respects finite capital. Human trade/intervention quota and emergency response availability still need definition. Monitoring 24/7 does not mean all contracts trade 24/7.
- Free data/resources preferred; no foreign cards/phones, small justified one-off spending may be considered. Finest FREE defensible timeframe is a research question, not an assumed one-minute/live entitlement. Dark-only future screener and Telegram alerts. MacBook Pro Intel 6-core i7, 16 GB RAM, Radeon Pro 5300M 4 GB. VPS: 1 vCPU, 1 GB RAM + 2 GB swap, 20 GB disk, active VPN and other workload; 250–350 MB background budget is shared and unverified. ## Question
Can a small manual VTB futures-spread pilot on the same account as LQDT be controlled without implicitly exposing the entire core portfolio, and how would BCS differ? Treat qualified-investor status and KPUR as separate dimensions, not synonyms or automatic discounts.

## Investigate
1. Build a dated authority chain: applicable Bank of Russia regulation, MOEX/NCC rules, each broker's agreement/risk disclosure/tariff/risk-rate methodology and account-specific settings. Separate exchange participant requirements from retail client rules. Trace which rule prevails where they differ.
2. Compare recognized client categories, accessible instruments/modes, initial/maintenance requirements, broker add-ons, open-risk limits, portfolio/same-underlying/calendar offsets, removal of offsets, and gross unhedged-leg requirements during entry/exit. Distinguish futures long/short positions from securities borrowing.
3. Establish LQDT eligibility, haircut/valuation currency, cash needed for negative VM, settlement lags, settlement-account cash vs securities collateral, automatic sales/transfers, claims against other assets/subaccounts, lending interest if cash goes negative, and opt-out/segregation possibilities. “Same broker” does not prove “same collateral pool”; request written account-scope confirmation.
4. Explain margin recalculation/intraday changes, liquidation triggers, broker discretion, notification channel/timing, grace periods IF documented, close-out sequencing and remaining liability. Never assume enough time for 90-minute human response.
5. Assess implications of accessing from a Polish VPS and user jurisdiction without inferring citizenship or tax residence. Only document operational restrictions relevant to this project.
