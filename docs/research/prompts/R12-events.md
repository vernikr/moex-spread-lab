## Investigate
1. Official issuer disclosures, board recommendations, shareholder decisions, ex-/record/payment dates, exchange/clearing adjustments and index methodology inputs. Different event types/dates are not interchangeable. Include cancellations/revisions, special dividends and changes in share/contract specifications.
2. Distinguish realized event history from forecasts known at trade time. A current polished dividend calendar may contain future knowledge; build provenance/version/available-at requirements. Analyst forecasts, if used, need timestamped vintages and rights.
3. Required carry inputs by family: rates/funding, dividend expectations, index weighting and FX, with appropriate source history and synchronization. Corporate-action omissions can look like profitable mean reversion.
4. Free access/depth, revisions, scraping/license limitations, archive stability, missing-document handling and plausible historical backfill. Absence of reliable historical versions may restrict the hypothesis to forward collection.
5. Prepare stage-7 extraction criteria for the user's prior projects: source connectors, raw snapshots, schemas, date semantics, correction history, code/tests, known mistakes/dead ends and licenses. Do not assume any old code/data has been supplied or is correct.
