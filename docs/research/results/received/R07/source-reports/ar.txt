# R07 — Pilot risk, funding liquidity, stress and sizing

**Question:** Are the delegated pilot defaults internally coherent and feasible for integer futures spreads with 3–90 minute reaction, and what bounded sizing/risk rules should be tested?

**Research date:** 2026-10-14 (current-facts cutoff; source pack prepared 2026-10-07; all market figures from the 07.10.2026 ISS snapshot inside the supplied reports unless dated otherwise).

**Status of this document:** evidence-backed research report. Not investment advice, not a trading recommendation, not a legal or contractual opinion, not a backtest. All ledgers are hypothetical arithmetic on supplied parameters. No account was accessed, no trades were placed, no broker was contacted, no books were read from the user's library in this investigation.

---

## 0. Executive verdict

**The delegated defaults are arithmetically coherent as a research-governance system, but they are not, by themselves, feasible as a live risk-control system at RUB 30,000 with 3–90 minute manual reaction on a shared account.** Four findings drive this verdict:

1. **The 5,000 ₽ gap between the action trigger (10k) and the outer boundary (15k) is not defensible as a universal buffer.** Per-pair daily σ (post-23.03.2026 window) is 688 ₽ (CNYRUBF/CRZ6), 531 ₽ (GLDRUBF/GLZ6), 1,614 ₽ (IMOEXF/MMZ6) [R06 §12.1], so the gap spans 7.3 σ-days on a single CNY pair but only **1.5 σ-days on 2 IMOEX pairs, 1.03 on 3 IMOEX pairs, 1.45 on 5 CNY pairs, 1.9 on 5 GL pairs** — i.e. ~1–2 σ-days for any book at the top of a feasible profile. With documented reaction chains of 18–105 minutes (R04) and no guaranteed broker grace period (R01), a fixed 5k gap can be consumed inside one adverse session on medium-vol books. **Replace the fixed gap with a computed uncertainty buffer `u` (delay-window stressed σ + exit costs), retain 5k only as a floor.**
2. **The binding constraint is not margin and not commissions — it is the stressed VM/cash path plus the LQDT counterfactual.** A 2–5-pair book fits the 50%-of-budget margin ceiling easily on NCC gross GO, yet one joint shock (price gap + spread-offset removal near expiry + reaction delay) can move required liquidity by 8–15k ₽ in a morning. Conversely, in the "cash sleeve" accounting the carry component of the flagship pairs does **not** reliably clear the matched-LQDT hurdle (~14% p.a.) at 30k scale without convergence-timing alpha whose evidence base (141 daily observations) is inconclusive (R09).
3. **Internal limits cannot bound the account-wide tail.** LQDT is admitted collateral at both brokers (VTB risk list 07.10.2026, row 813; BCS App. 6 §1.4), and forced closure is discretionary with no guaranteed notice (R01). A pilot-generated deficit that reaches the core portfolio is an incident, not a money bound: sale proceeds are not the loss, embedded-gain tax on core lots can be material, and the 45k gross-contribution cap is explicitly **not** a loss ceiling (worked Ledger C: X = −35,100 ₽ inside a 35,100 ₽ gross envelope, per R05 §6.12a logic). Live trading therefore stays gated on the five written broker confirmations of R01 (isolation of the margin perimeter, KPUR, LQDT haircuts, notification channel, absence of clause-15/19 derogations) — until then **no-trade on real money is the coherent default**, and the pilot proceeds as a capital-limited virtual portfolio (permitted per R01 verdict).
4. **The delegated rules are action rules, not statistical bounds** (consistent with R09 §1: a 90-day pilot cannot establish profitability; illustrative t ≈ 0.5 at annualised excess Sharpe 1). Tiny-pilot profit cannot validate sizing, capacity or tail safety; promotion to 10–20m requires its own gates (§10).

**Recommended test package (to be tested, not adopted blind):** minimum integer lots only (1 pair = 1 unit 1:1-hedged); per-profile **σ-budget** in ₽/day as the primary size control (low 1.0k / medium 2.0k / high 3.5k ₽ per day 1σ, max 2 concurrent pairs / 3 pairs / 4 pairs, identical authority and identical outer boundary for all profiles); gross GO ≤ 33% of the remaining envelope **and** stressed GO (offset removed) + `u` ≤ envelope; entry/continuation inequality `X − L_stress − u ≥ −15,000` with `u = max(5,000, 2.5 × delay-window σ_book + exit costs)`; scale-ins only with precommitted per-idea aggregate caps; no Kelly-family sizing (probabilities uncalibrated — defer). Details in §4–§6.

## 2. Risk-state separation (Investigate 1)

The policy triggers on `X`/`D`; the broker triggers on portfolio solvency; markets trigger on price gaps. These are **different state variables measured in different systems** (R01 §7.2: "биржевой зачёт снижает стоимость позиции, а клиентский показатель НПР2 запускает ликвидацию — это два разных числа в двух разных системах"). The table keeps them separate:

| State dimension | Definition used here | Measured from | What it does NOT capture | Verified mechanics (07.10.2026) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Relative economic loss | `X = V − B`, `D = H − X` (R05 §2.4) | Synchronised wealth marks, matched external flows | Liquidity to survive the next VM call; broker's opinion | Policy layer only |
| 2. Absolute P&L | Wealth change net of external flows | Same ledger, second column | Benchmark drift; X can be negative with positive absolute P&L and vice versa (R05 §7.3) | Policy layer |
| 3. Peak drawdown of X | `D = H − X`, HWM convention fixed in advance (R05 §2.4: choose H-includes-current-X or `D⁺ = max(0, H − X)` and register it) | X series | Cash path; margin | Policy layer |
| 4. Cash/VM path | Free rubles + due VM + due fees + debt service vs time; VM settled **only in money** (BCS App. 6 §2.3 "Вариационная маржа – сумма денежных средств" [R01 §6.2]) | Daily clearing (ETS: mark 23:50–00:30, member margin call 09:00/10:00, execution to 17:30 [R01 §7.1]) | Economic X (a liquidity crisis can precede X = −15k) | Verified |
| 5. Broker margin/collateral | M0 = ГО × k; NPR1 = S − M0 − S_block; NPR2 = S − Mx; Mx = 0.5·M0; УДС = NPR2/(M0−Mx) (6681-У method; [R01 §8.3]) | Broker portfolio S (includes core LQDT at haircut) | Our internal 30k label — "внутренний остаток «30 000 ₽» не заменяет S брокера" [R01 §8.3] | Verified formula; account numbers unknown |
| 6. Gross exposure | Σ |leg notional|; integer contracts; n_pairs × pair notional (≈23–26k ₽ per flagship pair) | Trade log | Not a loss bound; gaps can exceed notional-relative σ | Market data verified |
| 7. Temporary single-leg risk | One leg filled/part-filled, the other stale/rejected; hedge "отменяется мгновенно при рассинхронизации ног" [R01 §9 conclusion 3] | Execution log (R08 domain) | Not visible in pair-level marks until the second leg is done | Mechanic verified; frequency unknown |

**Cross-state violations to highlight (live-trading blockers):**
- (i) Sleeve passes `X − L_stress − u ≥ −15,000` while broker NPR2 < 0 because core LQDT haircut rose (e.g., KPUR→KNUR swing on VTB: collateral credit of 30k units 27,600 → 24,600 ₽ [R01 §6.1]) — policy green, broker red.
- (ii) X ≈ 0 while cash path fails: VM due in money, LQDT sale settles T+1 (R05 §1.5/§6 conventions), negative balance is a separate paid product (VTB margin loan ≈ КС+6.9% = 20.9% p.a.; "ВТБ Мои инвестиции" snapshots [R02 §3.5]; BCS: deficit maintenance 0.1%/calendar day = ≈36.5%/yr simple [R01-55]) — "отсутствие найденной цены ≠ бесплатность" [R01 §8.3].
- (iii) Temporary naked leg (state 7) can convert a hedged pair into directional exposure larger than any X trigger's ability to respond within 3–90 minutes.

### 3.2 The two funding models change the hurdle by ~14% p.a. (must be chosen, not blurred)

The 30k label can be implemented two ways (R05 §4.3 allows either, same X algebra, different operations):

- **(a) Cash sleeve:** sell ≈30k of LQDT units, hold cash + futures. Then `B` keeps the units and `X` carries the full LQDT drift as opportunity cost: at RUSFAR ≈ key rate 14% (LQDT target is RUSFAR tracking, PDU 3915-17 [R05 §1.1]; key rate 14.00% since 27.07.2026 — cbr.ru), **the hurdle is ≈ 690 ₽ per 60 days ≈ 14% p.a. on the whole sleeve**.
- **(b) Pledged units + small VM cash buffer:** keep units as collateral (haircut applies to credit, not to wealth — R05 §2.5/§6), sell only the VM buffer. Then X excludes LQDT drift on pledged units (they grow in both V and B) and instead pays: VTB fee on uncovered GO `(ГО − рубли) × (КС+2%) × T/365` = 16% p.a. currently ([R02 §3.5], key rate 14% + 2%; ≈61 ₽/month per 4.6k GO) and LQDT drift on the sold buffer. **Hurdle ≈ 2–4% p.a. on the sleeve**, but VM still needs real rubles and haircuts can bind.

**Consequence:** in model (a) the carry of the flagship pairs (CNY ≈ +3.1% p.a. on pair notional ≈ 13% p.a. on 30k with 5 pairs; GL ≈ +7.1% p.a. on notional ≈ 27% p.a. on 30k with 5 pairs — R06 §10.1–10.2) does **not** dominate the LQDT hurdle uniformly: CNY book is ≈ break-even before noise, GL book is positive in expectation but with daily 1σ = 2,655 ₽ (5 pairs) against a 690 ₽ hurdle. The strategy's excess depends on **spread-timing alpha (z-entry/exit)** and/or GL-family carry, not on the delegated risk arithmetic. This must be stated in the pilot charter: **zero-trade days are acceptable and a structurally mildly negative X vs LQDT is a plausible outcome of a correctly executed pilot.**

## 4. Entry/continuation inequalities and stress construction (Investigates 3–4)

### 4.1 Registered gates (all must hold; fail ⇒ no entry, no addition)

Let `RB` = remaining risk budget of the pilot envelope, `AL` = already-available liquidity, both recomputed at every gate with current marks.

1. **Outer-boundary gate (monetary):** `X − L_stress − u ≥ −15,000`, where
   - `L_stress` = stressed incremental economic loss of the **whole book after** the contemplated change, over the delay/exit window, under **joint** scenarios (price + margin + liquidity together), computed once (exit costs inside `L_stress` are not re-deducted from X — R05 §7.4 warning);
   - `u = max(5,000, 2.5 × σ_delay(book) + C_exit)` where `σ_delay(book)` = 1-day σ of the book's spread P&L in ₽ (1σ/day per pair from R06 §12.1: CNY 688, GL 531, IMOEX 1,614, GAZR ≈ 2.11% log-σ scaled, SBER 3.83% log-σ scaled) inflated for the reaction window (3–90 min + data chain) and for fat-tail days (use 2.5 as an explicit multiplier to be validated by R08 replay — a **test candidate**, not an approved constant), plus one-way exit costs (round-trip cost is 10.7–75.9 ₽/pair [R06 §12.1] plus half-spread crossing; GLDRUBF/G GLZ6 quoted 100–108 steps wide — limit orders only [R06 §10.1]);
   - with `u ≥ 5,000` the delegated heuristic is retained only as floor.
2. **Cash-path gate (independent of gate 1 — R01 §8.3 "два независимых запаса"):** free rubles (not LQDT credit, not unexecuted orders, not future proceeds with T+1 lag) ≥ `VM_stress(delay window) + fees + debt service + settlement-lag reserve`. VM is money-only [R01 §6.2]; LQDT sale proceeds arrive T+1 [R01 §6.2, R05].
3. **Margin gates:** gross GO (sum of both legs, NCC basis, broker add-on allowance) ≤ 33% of remaining envelope (tightened from 50% — see sensitivity: 33% keeps ≥ 2σ VM reserve on the cheapest families); **and** stressed GO with spread offset **removed** (×2–3 near expiry per R06 §12.1, or the larger-leg rule) + `u` ≤ envelope. If the minimum lot fails either gate ⇒ **reject the idea** (no fractional contracts exist).
4. **Cluster gate:** concurrent pairs sharing a factor (RUB rate/carry; same underlying family; shared leg) count as one cluster; cluster 1σ/day ≤ 60% of the profile σ-budget. (R09 §2.3: overlapping positions change the evaluation object to the daily portfolio ledger.)
5. **Event gate (R06 §8.5):** no entry across known events on either leg (expected dividends table moex.com/s206, expiry, spec changes, IndexDiv = 0 cases for SBERF/GAZPF, TATN/TATP ex-date effects). Delivery/last-trading-day danger overrides all monetary triggers (policy).
6. **Authority gate:** entry fits the original 30k envelope after losses/reserves/commitments **with zero emergency funding**; past rescue money or retained earnings do not enlarge the entry budget (policy).

### 4.3 Testing the 50% margin hypothesis and the 5k buffer heuristic (Investigate 4)

Both are **test candidates**, not requirements. Test procedure (simulation on the registered 141-day window 23.03–07.10.2026 + scenario overlay, walk-forward per R09 — no significance claims from one window):

- **Margin ceiling ∈ {33%, 50%} of envelope**, always on **gross** GO, with a parallel **stressed-GO** column (offset removed). Report for each candidate pair set: min free cash, min (free cash − stressed VM), max D, trigger crossings. Pre-committed falsification: if any of {50% only, 33% only} admits a path that breaches the cash-path gate in S1–S5 while passing the margin gate, the margin gate alone is rejected as a control.
- **Buffer ∈ {5k fixed, computed u}.** Falsification: count path-days where realized adverse move over the measured reaction window exceeded the buffer in force. If the 5k-fixed version exceeds computed-u on ≥ 5% of stress days for a candidate book, retire 5k (expectation from §3.1: it fails for IMOEX-cluster books and passes for 2×GL). Gaussian tails are **not** assumed; use the empirical 141-day distribution plus S1–S6 multipliers; R09 §1 forbids treating any tail estimate as a guarantee.

## 5. Sizing rules to compare (Investigate 5)

All methods operate on **integer pairs** (1 pair = 1:1 unit hedge where the family allows — CNY, GL, GAZR, SBER, IMOEX per R06 §10; fractional hedging like RI/MIX is **excluded** at this scale [R06 §12.1]).

| Method | How it would work here | Verdict / test role |
|---|---|---|
| **(a) Fixed minimum contracts** (1 pair per idea, cap on concurrent ideas) | n = 1 per idea; caps: low 1 / medium 2 / high 3–4 concurrent | **Baseline; test as control.** Matches tiny-lot reality (Carver, §12: below ~$100k futures rounding dominates). Simple, transparent |
| **(b) Bounded risk-scaled sizing** | `n_i = min( floor(σ_budget / σ_i), floor(0.33·Env / GO_i), cap_i )` with σ_budget in ₽/day by profile; per-idea cap 2–3 | **Recommended candidate**; directly controls the binding constraint (VM path). σ_i from registered window; refresh at review |
| **(c) Capped fractional-Kelly-like** | Requires calibrated p(win), payoff ratio, edge per idea | **Defer.** R09: 141 observations, ~6–7 months of one regime, one expiry — cannot calibrate probabilities; "no 30-trade rule"; DSR/PBO are ceilings on optimism, not proofs. ## 8. The 90-day review convention and the peak-giveback rule (Required output)

### 8.1 Convention to register (exact)

- **Anchor:** `t0` = timestamp of the **first entry** in the live (or first funded virtual) pilot; if no entry occurs, review date is anchored at pilot start and reports "zero-trade period" (zero-trade is a valid outcome).
- **Review date** = `t0 + 90 calendar days` (example: start 2026-10-14 → review 2027-01-12). One review window of ±7 days for logistics; the thresholds and H/X/D/E ledgers are **continuous** and do not pause for the review.
- **At review:** re-verify §4.1 gates with fresh marks; re-estimate σ_i from the registered window plus new data; re-check broker parameters (haircuts, GO tables, fee schedules — they change: NCC LQDT params changed 2022→2026; VTB fee change 01.01.2027); publish X/H/D/E, incident list, reaction-time log, stale/partial-fill counts (R08 fields).
- **Positions are not force-closed at day 90.** A position may extend beyond review if its pre-registered idea horizon and exit conditions (e.g., convergence to a dated expiry like GLZ6 2026-12-17 — only 64 days after a 2026-10-14 start — or a registered z-target) still hold and the continuation gate passes. A **roll** (e.g., GLZ6→GLH7) is a **new entry** subject to all gates and the event filter (R06 §10.1 warns GLH7 has different liquidity/basis).
- **No silent resets:** X, H, D, E, realised losses and cumulative underperformance are never reset by review, funding, restart, withdrawal or renaming (R05 §4.6 — verified coherent against the policy).

### 8.2 Peak-giveback rule (D ≥ 10k) — opportunity cost

The D-trigger is a **high-water-mark ratchet**: with H = 12,000 it fires at X = +2,000 (positive X), suspends entries and demands risk reduction while the relative result is still positive (R05 §7.2 row 3). Two costs follow:

1. **Locked-out state:** if the book is cut to flat at X = 2,000, X can no longer improve; D stays ≥ 10,000 indefinitely; resume requires user review — a deliberate policy choice (protects against giving back wins) at the price of never re-risking without human action. **Recommendation to test:** a two-stage D-rule — at D ≥ 10k reduce risk by ~50% and suspend entries; only if the cash/margin gates also fail (or X ≤ −10k) go to flat. Counter-test: replay with the strict rule and measure missed-opportunity cost in the **virtual** ledger (the virtual portfolio exists to price exactly this).
2. **Foregone carry:** a 2-pair CNY book earns ≈ 263 ₽/60d carry (+GL ≈ 810 ₽/60d); being flat 60 days forgoes this relative to B. On the pilot's scale this is small versus the 10–15k acceptance band — the giveback rule's opportunity cost is second-order **at 30k**, but becomes first-order at 10–20m scale (§10), where de-risking a carry book is expensive. Register both effects in the review template.

**Interaction to flag:** D-trigger and X-trigger are independent by design (D fires on giveback, X on shortfall). A path 0 → +12k → −2k has D = 10k **and** X = −2k: both fire. This is intended conservatism; state it in the charter so a later operator does not "fix" it silently.

## 10. Criteria for no-trade (live or virtual entry)

Reject an idea or a day (record the rejection) if **any** hold:
1. Minimum lot fails §4.1 gates 1–3 (stress inequality, cash path, gross/stressed GO) — "if no minimum-lot spread fits, reject it" (prompt);
2. Live mode with any of R01's five broker confirmations missing (isolation, KPUR, LQDT haircut, notification channel, no derogation clauses);
3. Data layer unreliable: quote age > 30 min, one leg unsynchronised, settlement-vs-close ambiguity on marks (R04: `CLOSE ≠ SETTLEPRICE` in all 16 checked days of SiZ6);
4. Event window on either leg (R06 §8.5 filter): expected dividend dates (TATN 13.10.2026-style cases), expiry ≤ reaction window, spec change pending (2026 saw six equity-futures spec revisions), IndexDiv = 0 mismatch for SBERF/GAZPF;
5. Cluster cap exceeded (correlated concurrent book) or profile σ-budget exceeded;
6. E-allowance exhausted or a pending incident review (funding ≠ restart);
7. Monitoring/alerting path down (VPS budget 250–350 MB shared and unverified — prompt; blackout > delay window);
8. X/D in action state (no entries), or X ≤ −10k (exit-only);
9. Carry/sign structure flipped vs thesis (e.g., RGBIF funding sign instability 50/43 [R06 §10.5] — already a carry-reject).

**Zero-trade days and a zero-trade pilot are valid results.** Scarcity of opportunities is not evidence of safety (R09 §11: "Not allowed: converting scarcity into certainty").
