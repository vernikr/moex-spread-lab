Conditional evidence design; no measured incremental value or trading feasibility asserted.

**Conclusion:** As of 2026-10-23, incremental value is unmeasured: no baseline, historical dataset, broker confirmation or executed comparison was supplied or tested. Do not adopt a predictive regime layer yet. Preregister a small sequence of observable-state tests against two frozen no-regime spread families; defer hidden-state/change-point gating unless simple gates add robust out-of-sample net RUB excess over matched LQDT and leave enough independent episodes. A market closure is an eligibility rule, not evidence of predictive skill.

**Discard criteria:** Discard a regime variant if its paired incremental RUB excess is nonpositive on final holdout in base conditions, reverses under plausible stress costs/delays, breaks the provisional risk/funding authority, produces only one independent state/episode or too few opportunities for a credible uncertainty statement, relies on unavailable point-in-time inputs, or gains only by changing exits/size/pairs in addition to entry. Do not tune on the final holdout; insufficient sample means 'not demonstrated', not a positive finding. Stop the extension entirely if simple observable entry gates cannot demonstrate material robust improvement net of monitoring/intervention burden; absence of incremental evidence favors the no-regime baseline or no trading.

- Replay same 2022 closures with historically applicable notices; exchange closure/expiry/spec change is a common eligibility mask for baseline and gated variants, with entry impossible during closures and contemporaneous positions marked using explicitly stated valuation limits.
