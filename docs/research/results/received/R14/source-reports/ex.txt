**Ablation protocol:** Preregister contract universe, exact spread/basis definition and hypothesis horizons; unit is independently captured source item × first eligible decision time, not the document’s event date. Chronological rolling train→validate→test, purge overlapping forward outcomes and group syndicated/duplicate stories and adjacent legs/events; all dictionaries, source selection, labels, prompts, thresholds, scaling and missingness conventions fitted from then-available data. Log every tried feature/source and freeze one untouched final holdout; prospective capture is the principal clean test because a 2026 model cannot be a genuine 2020-era text model [Glasserman–Lin](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.17322) [Harvey–Liu–Zhu](https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/29/1/5/1843824). Ablate on IDENTICAL dates, contracts, eligibility and costs: A price-only; B A + deterministic dated formal corporate/financial flags and rate/flow/technical-event fields; C B + lexical Russian phrases/negation and numeric extraction; D C + tightly structured as-of model text; E placebo shuffled within a predeclared time block, delayed-text, and post-edit-only tests. Missing source means missing, not negative sentiment. Predefine model JSON: issuer, document ID, evidence quote with offsets, fact versus estimate/opinion, event/effective/publication times, entity/contract, change-vs-previous flag, confidence and abstain; reject unsupported quotes/identifiers, outside-context facts and generative trading advice. Disable external search/URL context, supply only cleared captured text and as-of previous comparison document, pin model and prompt/schema/temperature/config, store input/output hashes, model ID/version, response, usage and retries; repeats quantify nondeterminism. A later model’s hidden pretraining cannot be certified as historical knowledge: label retrospective inference exploratory, demand forward out-of-time replication.
